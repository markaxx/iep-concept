Le Filigare | Winery & Accommodation

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special On a Chianti ridge between Florence and Siena, Le Filigare is a hilltop wine estate with two stone hamlets, a panoramic pool and horizon views in every direction. Olive groves line the drive; terraces and lawns open straight onto the vines. The feel is rustic-elegant and calm, with tastings and cellar moments woven into the stay.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. Apartments and suites sit across the borgo, so your core group stays on site while everyone else lodges nearby. As planning benchmarks, on-site beds reach roughly 60–70 across 14 apartments, which keeps the weekend close and unhurried. Florence Airport sits about 45 minutes away; Pisa is another easy gateway. We coordinate rooming lists, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Say your vows on the lawn with the vineyards behind you, or on the terrace at golden hour. Then slide into cocktails by the pool and a long-table dinner under lights. If the forecast shifts, we pair an elegant indoor plan: the glass-framed Veranda or the vaulted hall (Sala delle Volte) keep the view and the mood. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers—so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Outdoors, receptions scale to around 90–100 guests depending on the terrace and lawn used. Indoors, the Veranda and Sala delle Volte provide refined Plan-B options for dinner and dancing. Ceremony: view lawn or terrace. Aperitivo: pool terrace. Dinner: courtyard, terrace or Veranda for a seamless flow with minimal moves. We right-size floor plans to protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The estate spans about 60 hectares with organic vines and olives, so tastings and walks start at your door. The pool sits a short stroll from the main hamlet, which makes day-after lounging effortless. Nearby churches in San Donato in Poggio suit a Catholic mass; civil rites can be arranged locally while we keep the reception at the estate. We manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout.

Accommodation: 14 apartments across two hamlets – maximum capacity of 70 guests

Max wedding guests: Up to 100 guests
