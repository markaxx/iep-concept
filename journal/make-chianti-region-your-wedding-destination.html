Make Chianti Region your Wedding Destination!

Tuscany4 March 2017

Dear Couples, look at these beautiful Chianti views…

Chianti is a region in between Florence and Siena, known all over the world for the famous Chianti wine, for its landscapes, vineyards...

The Chianti area is in the heart of Tuscany; going across the Via Chiantigiana, smoothly passing through the Chianti Classico zone, starting from Florence, the pearl of Tuscany, you can reach Siena, known in the world for the famous "Palio di Siena", a horse race of ancient origins, in about an hour and a half.

The via Chiantigiana runs for about seventy kilometers, in a unique landscape full of villages, villas, castles, abbeys, wine bars and houses of wine producers.

Olive trees and vineyards follow one another almost without interruption, creating games of stunning shades of green and offering emotions at any time of the day, from dawn to sunset. Whenever you decide to marry, being it in spring, summer, fall or winter, you’ll find a special scenery for your special wedding!

In all this beauty, you’ll surely find the perfect venue for your fabulous wedding, being it a villa, a hotel or why not, a castle!
