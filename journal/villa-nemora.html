Villa Nemora

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: tucked into the Valdambra near Bucine, Villa Nemora sits among Chianti’s olive groves, woodlands and vineyards with the romance of a private hamlet. Historic farmhouses with creamy stone walls open to cypress-lined gardens and airy, arched salons, creating an easy indoor–outdoor flow for cocktails, dinner and dancing. It feels quietly glamorous yet relaxed, with the countryside unfolding in every direction

Ideal for: a multi-day destination wedding where everyone settles in together. The estate is composed of separate homes—most notably Villa Felciai and Villa Fabbri—plus smaller apartments, giving you the flexibility to host different moments across the property. On site you can sleep a large group across the houses, and events scale beautifully outdoors to around 100 guests while keeping a house-party vibe.

Wedding experience: symbolic ceremonies set on the lawns or terraces, with civil or religious rites arranged in nearby town halls and churches. The in-house team can handle catering and the practicalities—from cake to music, flowers and light design—so the weekend flows from a welcome dinner to a pool day and the main event without rushing. Supplier choices can be flexible where needed

Good to know: Villa Felciai lists 11 bedrooms and bathrooms and a private pool; Villa Fabbri has 10 bedrooms with its own pool and travertine terraces; the smaller apartments add extra beds so your core group can stay on site. As a planning benchmark, La Lista notes accommodation for about 58 across three accommodations, an indoor wet-weather solution for roughly 70 and a guest maximum of about 100. The location sits between Siena and Arezzo, roughly 1.5 hours from Florence airport, keeping travel smooth for international guests.

Accommodation: Sleeps 24 + 26 + 8 in three separate accommodations

Max wedding guests: Maximum 100 wedding guests
