Castello La Leccia

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special High above Castellina in Chianti, Castello La Leccia is an 11th-century castle turned wine retreat. Terraces, Italian gardens and a panoramic pool open to rolling vines and olive groves. Inside, the restoration keeps historic character while feeling effortless and modern. You’re minutes from medieval towns, yet the mood stays calm and rural.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. Your inner circle stays on site in elegant rooms and suites; other guests sleep nearby in Chianti. Between moments, the organic winery and tasting rooms add a true sense of place. Travel is simple via Florence and Pisa. We coordinate rooming, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Exchange vows in the courtyard or among the gardens with the hills as your backdrop. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. If the forecast shifts, indoor salons and covered areas keep the timeline smooth without losing atmosphere. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Outdoors, dinners flow beautifully on the gravel terraces and garden lanes; the courtyard suits ceremonies, cake and first dance. Indoors, connected rooms give you an elegant shoulder-season plan. We right-size floor plans to your guest list and season, reducing moves and protecting sightlines for photos.

Good to know The pool is panoramic and sits just below the Italian garden, which makes sunset portraits easy. On free mornings, guests can join wine tastings, cooking classes, bike tours or even truffle hunts across Chianti. We manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout, so the weekend stays unhurried and beautifully paced

Accommodation: The Castle has 12 spacious rooms for up to 24 people and The Dependance has 5 modern rooms for up to 10 people

Max wedding guests: 60 guests
