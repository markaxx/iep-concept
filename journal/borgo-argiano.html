Borgo Argiano

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special In the southern Chianti Classico, Borgo Argiano is a restored hilltop hamlet surrounded by woods, vines and olive groves. Stone courtyards, a panoramic pool and modern lounges open to long views. The feel is refined yet relaxed—country elegance a short hop from Siena.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. Apartments and suites sit across the borgo, so your inner circle can stay on site while other guests sleep nearby. You are just 12 km from Siena, which keeps transfers, portraits and church options simple. We coordinate rooming lists, transport and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic or civil ceremony in the gardens or on the lawn with the hills behind you. Then ease into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights in the courtyard. If the season asks for cover, we pair an elegant indoor plan that still feels connected to the view. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers—so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Ceremony: garden lawn or courtyard. Aperitivo: pool terrace or upper garden. Dinner: courtyard and gravel terraces for alfresco layouts; the wine lounge and connected interiors for a seamless Plan B. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos. (Exact capacities vary by layout and season; we’ll tailor them to your guest count.)

Good to know The estate runs tastings and winery experiences with its sister cantina, Tenuta Cappellina—ideal for a welcome evening or a slow day-after. Walking and cycling routes start at the gates, and Siena sits twenty minutes away for quick excursions. We manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout, keeping the weekend calm and beautifully paced

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 22 people in 11 apartments, plus the Grand Suite for the newlyweds

Max wedding guests: Perfect for up to 100 guests
