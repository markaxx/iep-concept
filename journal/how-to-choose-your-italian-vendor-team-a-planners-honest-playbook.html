How to choose your Italian vendor team: a planner’s honest playbook

10 October 2025

Great teams share clarity and respect.

Start with the story you want to tell and the non-negotiables—guest experience, aesthetics, budget guardrails.

Ask vendors for recent full weddings in similar settings, not just highlights, and read contracts for scope, hours, backups and weather plans.

Insist on a primary contact and a response cadence.

If personalities mesh and logistics feel transparent, creativity will flourish without friction.
