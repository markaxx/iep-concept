Querceto di Castellina

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special High on a Chianti ridge above Castellina, Querceto di Castellina blends an organic winery with a warm agriturismo feel. Stone houses, a saltwater pool and a panoramic terrace look over the vines. In summer, the signature long table appears between the rows for vineyard dinners that feel cinematic yet relaxed.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. On site there are nine apartments plus the Livia double-suite, sleeping up to about 40 guests. Your inner circle can stay together on the estate; everyone else lodges nearby. Travel is simple between Siena and Florence. We coordinate rooming lists, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic ceremony on the terrace or at the vineyard edge. Then move into golden-hour aperitivo and a long-table dinner among the vines or by the pool. If you choose a legal civil rite, we schedule the ceremony at Castellina in Chianti’s medieval marriage hall and align timings with your celebration back at the estate. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and flow Querceto hosts only a handful of weddings per season—typically 6 to 8—so each weekend feels personal. Outdoors, dinners stretch along the vineyard lane for a striking, linear layout; indoors, apartments and lounges give you cosy spaces for getting-ready and a simple Plan B. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect the view.

Good to know Guests can add tastings, cellar visits and a dip in the saltwater pool between events. Pets are welcome, and parking is easy on site. We manage all timelines, paperwork and supplier teams, keeping the weekend calm and well-paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps 40 people in 9 apartments and 1 suite

Max wedding guests: Up to 80 guests
