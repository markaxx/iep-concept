Capri Palace Jumeirah – Luxury Wedding Venue in Anacapri, Capri

Amalfi Coast2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Capri Palace Jumeirah is one of the most luxurious wedding venues in Capri, offering an exclusive blend of modern design, Italian tradition, and unmatched luxury. Located in Anacapri, the quieter, more serene part of the island, the hotel offers breathtaking views of the Gulf of Naples and the island of Ischia, providing the perfect backdrop for a glamorous destination wedding on Capri. With its impeccable service and luxurious amenities, Capri Palace Jumeirah guarantees a truly unforgettable wedding experience.

Ideal for: Couples seeking a luxurious and elegant wedding in Capri, with a combination of five-star resort amenities, panoramic views, and exceptional service. This exclusive wedding venue is perfect for those looking for a private, glamorous celebration in one of the world’s most iconic Mediterranean destinations.

Wedding Experience: Say “I do” in the hotel’s beautiful garden, surrounded by lush greenery and panoramic views of Capri’s majestic coastline. For an even grander setting, exchange vows on the terrace, with stunning views of the Gulf of Naples and the island of Ischia as your backdrop. After the ceremony, enjoy a luxurious reception in the hotel’s sophisticated dining areas or on the terrace, overlooking the Mediterranean. Delight in gourmet cuisine, paired with fine wines from the region. The hotel’s world-class spa offers pre-wedding relaxation and pampering, ensuring the couple feels at their best on their special day.

With its exclusive atmosphere, stunning views, and unparalleled service, Capri Palace Jumeirah offers couples a luxurious Capri wedding experience that perfectly blends elegance, beauty, and Mediterranean charm.
