Best Wedding Venues in Sorrento, Italy: A Wedding Planner’s Guide

Amalfi Coast10 November 2026

Planning a destination wedding in Sorrento means choosing between some of the most beautiful wedding venues in southern Italy. Overlooking the Bay of Naples, with Mount Vesuvius on the horizon, this charming coastal town offers everything from historic villas and elegant luxury hotels to private estates surrounded by Mediterranean gardens.

But with so many beautiful Sorrento wedding venues to consider, how do you choose the one that truly suits your celebration?

At Italian Wedding Event Planners, we have been planning bespoke weddings in Italy since 2009. Our knowledge of Sorrento and its wedding venues allows us to guide international couples through the selection process, considering not only aesthetics but also guest experience, accommodation, accessibility and the practical details that make a destination wedding successful.

In this guide, we explore five distinctive wedding venues in Sorrento: Villa Astor, Capo Santa Fortunata, Bellevue Syrene, Villa Eliana and Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria. Each offers a different atmosphere and experience, helping you discover which setting best reflects your wedding vision.

Comparing the Best Wedding Venues in Sorrento

Choosing among the best wedding venues in Sorrento is not simply a matter of finding the most spectacular view. Each property offers a different combination of atmosphere, privacy, hospitality and event spaces.

Whether you envision a glamorous celebration overlooking the Bay of Naples, a romantic garden wedding or an elegant multi-day experience, understanding the character of each venue is the first step towards making the right choice.

Villa Astor — Historic Grandeur and Exceptional Sea Views

For couples drawn to architectural beauty and an exclusive atmosphere, Villa Astor is one of Sorrento’s most distinctive historic properties. Its gardens, terraces and extraordinary coastal setting create a sophisticated backdrop for a destination wedding.

Villa Astor is particularly worth considering for couples seeking the character of a private historic residence rather than a conventional hotel wedding experience.

Capo Santa Fortunata — Mediterranean Gardens and a Private Estate Atmosphere

Capo Santa Fortunata offers a different interpretation of the Sorrento wedding experience, combining Mediterranean greenery with beautiful views of the coastline.

Its estate atmosphere makes it an appealing option for couples who imagine a celebration surrounded by nature, with a relaxed yet elegant setting for their guests.

Bellevue Syrene — Refined Hospitality in the Heart of Sorrento

Bellevue Syrene combines the charm of a historic coastal property with the services and atmosphere of a luxury hotel.

For couples who appreciate elegant hospitality, sea views and the convenience of staying close to Sorrento’s historic centre, it is a venue worth exploring.

Villa Eliana — Intimate Elegance and Panoramic Surroundings

Villa Eliana offers a romantic setting characterised by Mediterranean landscapes and panoramic views.

It is particularly appealing to couples who prefer the atmosphere of a villa celebration and want to explore a more private, personalised wedding experience.

Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria — Timeless Luxury and Italian Heritage

Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria is one of Sorrento’s most recognisable luxury hotels, known for its historic character, gardens and spectacular setting overlooking the Bay of Naples.

It is an attractive choice for couples who envision a classic Italian wedding experience, combining an elegant celebration with the hospitality of an established luxury hotel.

What to Consider When Choosing a Wedding Venue in Sorrento

Beyond the beauty of a venue, planning a destination wedding in Sorrento requires careful consideration of several practical aspects.

Guest accommodation and accessibility are particularly important for international weddings. Some couples prefer a luxury hotel where guests can stay together, while others choose a private villa and arrange accommodation in nearby properties.

Transportation and logistics also deserve attention. Sorrento’s coastal roads, historic centre and surrounding landscape can make transfers an important part of the wedding experience, especially when guests are arriving from different countries.

For couples planning a multi-day celebration, it is equally important to consider how the venue fits into the overall itinerary, from welcome dinners and wedding-day celebrations to farewell gatherings.
