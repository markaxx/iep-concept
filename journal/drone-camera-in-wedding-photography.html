Drone camera in wedding photography

20 February 2015

Have you already heard about new trend in wedding photography? If not we are going to enlighten you.

A flying robot called Drone, which is used for aerial photography, will provide a new view on you r occasion. Drone allows capturing shots from an angle in which it is impossible unless you use a helicopter.

It is unpiloted aerial vehicle that managed by the remote control of a pilot on the ground. It gives an opportunity to shoot photos or videos from a distance while Drone is flying around. This new tendency is getting more and more popular. Now you are finally able to capture a full scale of your event.

Nevertheless, there are some limitations that you need to take into consideration before choosing a providing Service Company. It must be licensed and insured company, which provides service by skilled staff. In order to use it the weather should be good and calm. Drone’s battery is limited to 15 minutes, so usually length of footage worths around an hour. Drone is a great opportunity to capture your special moment in a special way. Beautiful, unique and creative shots/videos are guaranteed.
