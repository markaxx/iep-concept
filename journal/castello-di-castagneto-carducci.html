Castello di Castagneto Carducci

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: Above the hamlet of Castagneto Carducci, the castle’s terraces and gardens open to sweeping views of the Maremma and, on clear days, the Tuscan islands. Inside, restored salons keep their medieval character while feeling effortless for modern events. The on-site Church of San Lorenzo adds a consecrated, historic setting within the walls.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination celebration with a house-party rhythm. Stay spreads across the estate’s villas and hamlet houses, so your core group remains close while others lodge nearby. Travel is straightforward too: Pisa Airport is about 78 km and Florence roughly 130 km. We coordinate rooming plans, transfers and the full guest flow.

Wedding experience: Begin with vows in the Church of San Lorenzo or outdoors on a terrace. Then, move into golden-hour cocktails and an alfresco dinner under strings of lights. For scale, the Eastern Terrace seats about 190 and the Western Terrace around 110; the Garden of the Lecci suits up to roughly 150. If weather shifts, indoor rooms keep everything seamless: the Trophies Room seats up to 200, the Giardino dei Lecci Room about 80, the Princess Teresa Room about 70, the Red Room about 60 and the Count Ugolino Room about 50. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the aesthetic stays consistent from aisle to last dance.

Good to know: Terraces link directly to gardens, which keeps the day flowing without long moves. Moreover, the Conte Ugolino Court is a natural spot for cake, dessert and dancing. On clear evenings the west-facing outlook captures sunset over the sea, which reads beautifully in photos. Civil paperwork is typically handled at the local town hall, while church rites take place in San Lorenzo by parish agreement—we arrange both, together with timelines and supplier management.

Accommodation: 24 double rooms across a number of villas and houses, plus a suite for newlyweds at the castle

Max wedding guests: Up to 220 guests
