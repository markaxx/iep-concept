Where to Get Married on the Amalfi Coast

Ravello: Romantic Gardens and Timeless Elegance

Perched high above the Mediterranean, Ravello offers breathtaking panoramas, historic villas and an atmosphere of understated elegance.

Among its most celebrated wedding venues are Villa Cimbrone and Belmond Hotel Caruso, both renowned for their extraordinary settings.

Villa Cimbrone offers romantic gardens, historic architecture and spectacular views, while Belmond Hotel Caruso combines the elegance of a historic palazzo with sophisticated hospitality.

Ravello is particularly appealing to couples seeking a refined celebration surrounded by history, gardens and extraordinary coastal scenery.

Positano: Iconic Views and Mediterranean Glamour

With its pastel-coloured buildings cascading towards the sea, Positano is one of Italy’s most recognisable wedding destinations.

Its terraces, boutique hotels and intimate reception spaces provide a distinctive setting for romantic ceremonies and elegant celebrations.

Among the venues worth exploring are Villa Oliviero and Le Sirenuse, each offering a different interpretation of Positano’s unmistakable charm.

However, Positano also presents logistical challenges, particularly regarding transportation, accessibility and supplier coordination.

Working with an experienced wedding planning team can make a significant difference, ensuring that your celebration is as carefully organised as it is beautiful.

Amalfi: History, Coastal Beauty and Italian Atmosphere

Amalfi combines historic architecture, Mediterranean scenery and a lively Italian atmosphere.

From elegant hotels to panoramic terraces and historic settings, the town offers a variety of possibilities for destination weddings.

Choosing the right venue depends on your guest numbers, desired experience, accommodation needs and overall wedding budget.

The Importance of Local Expertise and Wedding Logistics

The Amalfi Coast is one of Italy’s most extraordinary wedding destinations, but its geography requires particularly careful planning.

Narrow coastal roads, limited vehicle access, seasonal traffic, historic venues and the movement of suppliers and guests all need to be considered well in advance.

A beautiful location is only one part of a successful wedding. Accessibility, accommodation availability, contingency plans and realistic event schedules are equally important.

Our experience allows us to evaluate these practical considerations from the earliest stages, helping couples choose venues that are not only beautiful but also suitable for their celebration.
