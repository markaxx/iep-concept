Casa2Elisabette

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special On the Cortona side of the Val di Chiana, Casa 2 Elisabette is a stone farmhouse wrapped in olives and open countryside. Views run wide, the architecture stays honest, and life moves easily between gardens, terraces and simple, characterful rooms. It feels intimate and personal—perfect for a slow, multi-day celebration.

Ideal for A house-party wedding with your inner circle staying on site. You can book one or both houses: the Villa sleeps up to ten and the Casa up to six, so sixteen guests in total is the sweet spot. Everyone else lodges nearby while we keep the core moments at the property. The venue shines with close-knit weddings up to about eighty guests. Travel is straightforward via Cortona with easy access to Montepulciano, Siena, Arezzo and Perugia.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic ceremony under the trees or on the lawn with the valley as your backdrop. Then ease into a golden-hour aperitivo and a long-table dinner under lights. If the weather turns, we pivot to the arched indoor spaces without losing atmosphere. For legal civil paperwork or a church mass, we arrange town-hall or parish rites in and around Cortona and align timings with your reception back at the farmhouse. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Plan for up to roughly eighty guests outdoors, using the natural levels to keep sightlines clear and moves short. For overnights, the Villa and the Casa give you distinct getting-ready zones and kid-friendly spaces, while the gardens, terraces and pool terrace become ceremony, cocktail and dinner settings with minimal transitions. We right-size the floor plan to season and guest count.

Good to know This is a calm, rural pocket a few minutes from Cortona, so portraits and quick excursions are easy. The farmhouse layout suits welcome pizza night, a relaxed pool day and a farewell brunch. Supplier policy is flexible; we manage everything end to end—rooming plans, transfers, production, paperwork and timelines—so your weekend stays unhurried and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 16

Max wedding guests: Up to 70 guests
