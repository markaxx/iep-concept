Tenuta Lupinari

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: Between Siena and Arezzo, Tenuta di Lupinari is a fairytale hamlet with a crenellated castle, gardens and a panoramic pool. Stone lanes and terraces open to rolling hills, so every moment stays close to the view. It feels intimate and authentic, yet purpose-built for celebrations

Ideal for: An intimate, multi-day destination wedding with your core group on site. There are 11 apartments plus 3 B&B rooms, sleeping up to about 43 guests. Larger parties stay nearby while we keep the house-party rhythm at the borgo. The venue shines with guest counts up to roughly 60.

Wedding experience: You can hold a legal civil ceremony on the property under the castle tower. Then slide into golden-hour cocktails in the gardens and dinner under the lights. If needed, there are two indoor plan-B spaces of about 110 m² and 50 m², so the flow remains seamless in any season. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Good to know: Music outdoors typically ends at midnight; indoors it may continue until about 02:00, which suits a lively yet polished party. Fireworks aren’t permitted. The supplier policy is open, and you can opt for the in-house restaurant or external catering that we coordinate. Accessibility is limited across historic areas. For travel, Florence is about 1 hour 10 minutes and Pisa roughly 1 hour 52 minutes.

Accommodation: 11 apartments, 3 bedrooms B&B. Total of 22 beds with a maximum of 43 guests

Max wedding guests: Up to 60 guests
