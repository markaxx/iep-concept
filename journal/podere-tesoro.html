Podere Tesoro

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special On a ridge in Radicondoli, Podere Tesoro pairs a 17th-century farmhouse with a contemporary events hall. Terraces, lawns and a 22-metre infinity pool open to rolling hills. Inside, stone and glass keep the look rustic-modern. The estate also produces organic wines and olive oil, which adds a true sense of place.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. There are 10 bedrooms across the main villa, tower and annex, so your inner circle can stay on site. Everyone else lodges nearby while we keep the celebration centred at the estate. Travel is simple between Siena and San Gimignano.

Wedding experience Hold your ceremony outdoors with widescreen valley views. Then slide into golden-hour cocktails by the pool and a long-table dinner under lights. If you prefer full cover, we set the reception in the events barn with its panoramic windows. Should the forecast change, we pivot layouts without losing atmosphere. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and layout Outdoors, receptions scale comfortably for large guest counts across terraces and lawns. Indoors, the banquet hall seats 200+ and links directly to the gardens for an easy flow. Ceremony on the upper lawn, aperitivo by the pool, dinner in the hall or on the terrace—each moment has a clear zone, so moves stay short and photo-friendly.

Good to know Wellness is a signature here: expect a spa area with indoor plunge pool, hammam, sauna and a gym. On free mornings, guests can join tastings of the estate wines or relax on the sun terraces. We handle rooming plans, transfers, paperwork and production end to end, so the weekend feels calm and well-paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 22 people

Max wedding guests: Up to 200
