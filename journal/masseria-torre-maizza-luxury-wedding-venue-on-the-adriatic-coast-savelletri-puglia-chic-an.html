Masseria Torre Maizza – Luxury Wedding Venue on the Adriatic Coast, Savelletri, Puglia: Chic and Authentic Seaside Weddings

Puglia2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Nestled on the picturesque Adriatic coast, Masseria Torre Maizza offers a perfect blend of traditional charm and contemporary luxury. This stunning Puglia wedding venue features a beautiful pool, expansive gardens, and an exclusive private beach, providing a range of breathtaking backdrops for your special day. With its blend of authenticity and luxury, Masseria Torre Maizza is a sought-after destination for couples seeking a seaside wedding in the heart of Puglia.

Ideal for: Couples who desire a luxurious yet chic wedding by the sea, combined with the authentic charm of a traditional masseria. Whether you’re looking for an intimate ceremony in the lush gardens, a glamorous poolside exchange of vows, or a reception under the stars, this venue provides a versatile and stunning setting for your wedding in Puglia.

Wedding Experience: Say your vows surrounded by the serene beauty of lush gardens or next to the sparkling waters of the private pool. After the ceremony, celebrate your reception in one of the stylishly designed indoor spaces, or opt for a magical outdoor reception on the terrace, where you can enjoy stunning views of the landscapes and the Adriatic Sea. With luxurious accommodations, top-tier service, and a truly unique atmosphere, Masseria Torre Maizza promises an unforgettable seaside wedding experience in Puglia.
