La Pescaia Resort

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special In the Maremma near Grosseto, La Pescaia Resort centers on 19th-century Villa Tolomei, wrapped by ancient olive groves, pines and wide lawns. Gardens, courtyards and a long pool keep the view in every scene. The feel is rustic-elegant and serene, with nature all around

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. On site, the estate sleeps roughly 34–45 guests across rooms and adjacent villas, depending on setup; overflow stays nearby while we keep events at the resort. Travel is straightforward via Pisa, Florence or Rome

Wedding experience Exchange vows under the pines or beside the olive trees. Then move into golden-hour cocktails by the pool and a long-table dinner on the lawn or courtyard. If the season asks for cover, we pair the plan with indoor options—such as the Granaio and Orangerie—so the mood stays seamless. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers

Capacities and layout As planning benchmarks, the resort notes about 6,000 m² of parks and gardens and roughly 300 m² indoors. Outdoors, receptions comfortably scale toward 150–200 depending on layout; indoors, seated plans of ~70–90 work well across the linked halls. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos

Good to know You’re minutes from Sticciano Scalo and within easy reach of Siena and the Maremma coast. Between events, guests can swim, join tastings or simply wander the estate’s paths through olives and woodland. We manage rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and production end to end, so the weekend stays calm and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 44 guests (possible to reach 50 guests with 6 extra beds)

Max wedding guests: 100-120 people
