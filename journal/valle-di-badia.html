Valle di Badia

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the Pisan hills near Buti, Valle di Badia is a restored 18th-century hamlet with a private chapel, gardens and a scenographic pool at its heart. Stone barns and lawns create natural stages for each moment, while woodland and streams keep the setting secluded and green. You’re about 30 minutes from Pisa Airport, yet the mood is timeless and rural.

Ideal for: A multi-day, house-party celebration with your inner circle on site. The estate offers ten apartments plus a bridal suite, sleeping up to roughly fifty guests; bookings run exclusively through the main contract, which keeps everything coordinated. Larger guest lists are easy to accommodate nearby while we keep the core events in the borgo.

Wedding experience: Say your vows in the stone chapel or outdoors by the pool and lawns. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. For scale, the ancient barns and terraces comfortably host large receptions, with marquee options if you want full weather cover. Civil or symbolic rites can happen on site; for a Catholic mass, we arrange nearby churches and manage the timeline so the flow stays smooth. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Good to know: Spaces include the chapel forecourt, gardens, pool deck and historic barns, so you can switch scenes without long transfers. The hamlet sits between Lucca, Florence, Siena and Livorno, which makes guest excursions simple. Moreover, the estate has won regional wedding awards and is purpose-built for events, from back-of-house to guest paths. We handle rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and production end to end.

Accommodation: Apartments sleeping up to 50 people

Max wedding guests: Up to 180 guests
