Borgo Siliano

Umbria8 October 2025

Why it’s special On the Umbrian–Tuscan border near Città della Pieve, Borgo Siliano (Antico Podere Siliano) is a family-run estate dating to the late 1800s. Vineyards, olive trees and wide lawns surround stone houses and a panoramic pool. The mood is authentic and calm—perfect for a slow, multi-day celebration.

Ideal for A house-party wedding with your inner circle staying on site. As planning benchmarks, the estate sleeps roughly 40–45 guests and can host events up to about 150 outdoors. Between moments, we weave in relaxed experiences—pizza night, pasta night, vineyard dinner or a pool-day brunch—to keep the rhythm easy.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic or civil ceremony on the lawns overlooking the valley. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. If the weather shifts, we pivot to linked indoor spaces and elegant cover without losing atmosphere. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Ceremony: view lawn by the cypress line. Aperitivo: pool terrace or upper garden. Dinner: courtyard or garden for alfresco layouts; adjacent interiors as a refined Plan B. As a guide, think ~45 sleeping on site and up to ~150 for the main night outdoors. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The estate produces organic wine and olive oil, so tastings feel natural. You’re about 1 km from the Tuscan border, with easy day trips to Montepulciano, Pienza and Lake Trasimeno. We manage timelines, transport, paperwork and production end to end, keeping the weekend calm and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 56 guests in the following: 6 apartments, 3 double rooms, 2 eco villas

Max wedding guests: up to 150 guests
