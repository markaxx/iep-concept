Antico Borgo La Torre

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special Set in the Le Balze nature reserve near Reggello, Antico Borgo La Torre is a historic farm estate with vineyards, olive trees and a private lake. Gardens lead to a 20-metre pool that looks over the water. Two main buildings and outbuildings frame courtyards and long views, just 25 km from Florence. It feels rustic, authentic and quietly elegant.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. The borgo offers several self-contained apartments for your inner circle, so everyone settles in. Meanwhile, extra guests stay nearby and join for each event. Short moves, simple logistics, and a calm countryside rhythm.

Wedding experience Exchange vows by the lake or beside the pool at golden hour. Then move to cocktails under the vine-and-wisteria pergola and a long-table dinner as the hills turn pink. If the weather shifts, we pivot indoors to the restored granary halls without losing atmosphere. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and layout • Pergola: 155 m²; catering up to ~140. • Vault Hall (ground floor): 120 m²; catering up to ~110. • Truss Hall (first floor): 144 m²; catering up to ~130. These spaces link directly to lawns and courtyards, so the flow is easy: ceremony by the water, aperitivo at the pool, dinner under the pergola or in the halls, and dancing after dark. We right-size floor plans to protect sightlines and keep transitions short.

Good to know The 32-hectare estate includes vineyards, olive groves and wide gardens. Apartments are independent and car-free inside the hedged area, which keeps the setting peaceful. Florence is a simple hop for guest arrivals and day trips. We manage rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and production end to end.

Accommodation:There are 9 spacious apartments with a total of 17 bedrooms with respective bathrooms, accommodating up to 36 guests. All aparments have a living room with dining area and a fully equipped kitchen. Most apartments have a fire place. There is no air-conditioning, but the bedrooms are fan-cooled and designed with architecture that helps keep them naturally cool.

Max wedding guests: Up to 130 guests
