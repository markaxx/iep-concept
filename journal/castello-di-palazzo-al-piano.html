Castello di Palazzo al Piano

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: nestled on the edge of the Montagnola Senese near Siena, Palazzo al Piano is a medieval castle with 12th-century roots and a crenellated tower that surveys classic Tuscan hills. The estate blends heritage architecture with contemporary comforts and more than 15,000 m² of lawns, terraces and courtyards, so every moment—from the welcome cocktail to the cake—unfolds in a setting that feels cinematic yet private. Its position makes day trips to Siena, Florence and the Abbey of San Galgano effortless.

Ideal for: couples planning a multi-day destination celebration with both scale and flexibility. Outdoors, the venue can host up to about 300 guests; indoors, the Plan B hall of roughly 300 m² seats around 150–160. On-site and nearby beds total about 25 (typically 12 in the castle and 13 in Borgo Casine), supplier choice is open, and music can run until 1:00 a.m. with the option to extend to 3:00 a.m. Siena lies roughly 17 km away, with Florence about 80 km.

Wedding experience: civil ceremonies and selected religious blessings can be held on site among stone courtyards, panoramic gardens and elegant salons, with the team curating pizza nights, barbecues, brunches and tastings around the main day for a relaxed house-party vibe. The flow works beautifully in any season, moving from alfresco aperitifs to dinner and dancing with a seamless indoor fallback, supported by dedicated hospitality spaces and suites named after Tuscan masters.

Accommodation: 25 sleeping accommodation in total (12 beds in the castle, 13 beds in the Borgo Casine)
