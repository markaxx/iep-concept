Relais Villa Olmo

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: On the Chianti hills in Impruneta, Relais Villa Olmo pairs a 16th-century farmhouse with vineyards, olive groves and a panoramic pool. Terraces and stone loggias open to long valley views, while the Diadema restaurant and wine spirit root the experience in place. You are just twenty minutes from Florence, yet the mood stays quietly rural and refined.

Ideal for: An intimate, multi-day celebration with your inner circle staying on site. As a planning benchmark, independent venue guides note sleeping capacity up to about 47 across rooms, suites and private villas. Larger guest lists are easy to accommodate nearby while we keep the house-party rhythm at the relais. Travel is simple via Florence, with Pisa as a second option.

Wedding experience: Hold a symbolic ceremony in the gardens or by the pool at golden hour. Then move into cocktails on the terrace and a candlelit dinner beneath the loggias or in the glass-fronted veranda. For a legal civil rite, we arrange town-hall ceremonies in Florence or the local comune; for a Catholic mass, the Basilica di Santa Maria all’Impruneta sits nearby. We curate catering, music, lighting, styling and timelines with our trusted suppliers, so the flow stays seamless.

Good to know: Accommodation mixes classic rooms, junior and garden suites, family suites and villas, so we can zone guests by needs and schedules. Onsite amenities include the panoramic pool, a small spa feel, free parking and pet-friendly policies. Between events we build relaxed moments—tastings, cooking experiences and easy excursions into Florence—so the weekend feels like a shared holiday.

Accommodation: Sleeps 44 guests, with the addition of the sofa beds, 47 guests can be accommodated

Max wedding guests: Ideal for up to 50 guests
