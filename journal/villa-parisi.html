Villa Parisi

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: poised on the cliffs of Castiglioncello with the sea at its feet, Villa Parisi is a Belle Époque villa-hotel with private access to the water, a panoramic pool and terraces that read like cinema stills at sunset. It’s one of the few hotels here with its own path to the sea, and the glass-fronted veranda gives you an all-weather, sea-view dining room without losing the atmosphere. The result is a dreamy Tuscan-coast setting that feels both glamorous and effortless.

Ideal for: couples who want a seaside destination wedding with everyone close by. On site there are around 21 guest rooms sleeping roughly 45, so your inner circle can stay at the villa; for events, covered seating scales comfortably up to about 150. Pisa airport is the natural gateway at roughly 45 km, making guest logistics smooth.

Wedding experience: you can host a civil or symbolic ceremony on the property, from the sea-view terraces to the wooden platform set above the rocks, then move to cocktails by the pool and dinner beneath the veranda lights. A nearby church suits religious rites and Castello Pasquini in town is an option for municipal ceremonies. The in-house kitchen prepares fresh, seasonal menus with the possibility of a tasting dinner ahead of time, and the team can add playful touches like open bar, gelato cart, champagne fountain or a late-night pizza party to keep the celebration flowing.

Accommodation: The Villa features 21 rooms (18 double rooms and 3 triple rooms) with a maximum capacity of 45 guests

Max wedding guests: Up to 130 guests
