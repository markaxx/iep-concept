Borgo Bastia Creti – Rustic Wedding Venue in Assisi, Umbria: A Romantic Medieval Village Surrounded by Olive Groves and Vineyards

Umbria2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Nestled near Assisi in the heart of Umbria, Borgo Bastia Creti is a stunning medieval village surrounded by lush olive groves and vineyards, offering panoramic views of the serene Umbrian countryside. This historic venue combines rustic charm with modern luxury, creating a perfect setting for a romantic countryside wedding. Its peaceful surroundings and unique blend of traditional elegance and contemporary luxury make it an ideal choice for couples seeking a destination wedding in Umbria.

Ideal for: Couples dreaming of a rustic, romantic wedding set in the idyllic countryside of Umbria, with the added bonus of being close to the cultural and spiritual heart of Italy. Whether you envision an intimate ceremony in the courtyard or a garden wedding, Borgo Bastia Creti offers the perfect atmosphere for a relaxed yet luxurious celebration. The venue’s combination of traditional elegance and modern comfort makes it an ideal choice for couples looking for a rustic wedding venue in Italy with close proximity to Assisi.

Wedding Experience: Say your vows in the Borgo’s charming courtyard or in its beautiful gardens, surrounded by nature and offering breathtaking views of the Umbrian hills. After the ceremony, enjoy a reception in the rustic dining room or on the outdoor terrace, where guests can savor an authentic Italian feast paired with the finest local wines. With its peaceful atmosphere and stunning countryside setting, Borgo Bastia Creti offers the ideal location for a relaxed, intimate wedding in Umbria, making it the perfect destination for couples seeking a romantic and memorable celebration.
