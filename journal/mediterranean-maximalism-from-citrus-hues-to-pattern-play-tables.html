Mediterranean maximalism: from citrus hues to pattern-play tables

10 October 2025

Maximalism in Italy is less about clutter and more about joy.

Start with a confident palette—lemon, marine, coral—and let stripes, tiles and hand-painted ceramics converse rather than compete.

Anchor the table with one patterned element, then layer solids and natural textures to keep the eye calm.

Florals can be fruit-forward and sculptural, with branches, citrus and herbs adding scent and silhouette.

Balance bold color with generous negative space, especially on dinnerware, so food remains the hero.

The result feels sun-kissed and editorial without tipping into theme-park territory.
