Relais Castel Bigozzi

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: On a ridge near Monteriggioni’s walls, Relais Castel Bigozzi is a medieval castle turned 4-star relais. Stone courtyards, lawns and a panoramic pool open to Chianti views. Inside, you’ll find warm Tuscan rooms, a small spa and a glass-framed veranda that looks onto olive trees and, in the distance, San Gimignano. It feels historic yet easy, perfect for a slow, multi-day celebration.

Ideal for: A house-party wedding where your core group stays on site. The relais has 17 rooms with about 44 beds, so your inner circle can settle in; everyone else sleeps nearby in Strove and Monteriggioni. Travel is simple: Florence is roughly 55–70 minutes, Pisa about 1 hour 25.

Wedding experience: Hold a symbolic ceremony in the gardens, then move to golden-hour cocktails by the pool and dinner under the veranda or on the lawns. For a legal civil rite, we arrange ceremonies at Monteriggioni’s Sala Dante Alighieri or at Abbadia a Isola; for a Catholic mass, local churches around Strove and Monteriggioni are close at hand. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the flow stays seamless.

Good to know: Outdoor guest capacity scales up to about 300; indoors, the covered restaurant/veranda provides an elegant weather back-up. On site there are 17 rooms, a travertine-edge pool and a small spa. Catering is in-house, and on-site ceremonies are symbolic only. A minimum two-night exclusive stay is a sensible baseline. Meanwhile, the venue notes step-friendly access across key areas. We manage timelines, paperwork and church liaisons end to end.

Accommodation: The venue is composed 17 rooms, which has 44 beds
