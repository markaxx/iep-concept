mamma mia...recipes and flavors Italiani!!!

Amalfi Coast8 April 2013

In Ravello- ITALY

The world famous city of music, history and enchanting sights. Overlooking the Amalfi Coast.

The cooking course is held in a state-of-the-art outdoor kitchen on a terrace in Ravello. The town of Ravello is located high above the Amalfi Coast, with views that stretch to Capri and beyond. It is a town famous for its music and culture as well as gastronomic events.

This is a " demonstration " cooking course where you will learn about the natural ingredients used, which are fresh picked the very same morning of each class. The Italian garden is located below the terrace, so each participant can also learn about growing the organic vegetables. Pick your vegetables from her kitchen garden, learn how to make your own Limoncello, savour a delicious meal, and listen to Mamma's stories about cooking for celebrities.

After the class, participants share the beautiful meal prepared, and enjoy the local wines of the Amalfi region. The experience of the course will enable you to cook for your family, organize dinners at home with a rustic Italian flavor. Above all, it will furnish the knowledge of natural Mediterranean diet which is one of the healthiest in the world.
