A wonderful Italian Experience: Discover the beauty of Florence, Tuscany, Cinque Terre & Rome

A 11 day trip to Italy

A custom tour designed by Italian Event Planners for you to discover the beauty of Italy!. Enjoy different landscapes selected for their beauty and scenery. Discover the art of Renaissance Florence. The stunning countryside and wines of Tuscany. The cliff-hugging villages of the Cinque Terre. The Eternal City of Rome. Delicious local cuisine and excursion off the beaten track walking.

Details

Departures: Flexible departure date.

Price: The price starting from Euro 1900 per person, based on double occupancy on a beautiful 3 star small boutique Hotels during a low season. The price will vary depending on hotels & travel dates. Please contact us for a more customized tour & quotation.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1:

Welcome to Italy! Your private local driver will pick you up at the airport of Florence to your beautiful hotel with a stunning setting and breathtaking view right on the south bank of the Arno just a few steps from the Ponte Vecchio and just in the historical center of Florence. You will experience precious furnishings and paintings by Picasso and Cocteau blend with chic, contemporary styling and natural light that pours through the tall windows. From the top floor you can see Brunelleschi’s Dome … you could watch the river flow from your private balcony with a glass of Chianti Classico and dine in Borgo San Jocopo, the hotel restaurant, with more lovely views and a gorgeous riverside terrace. The food is pure Italian with added creativity. Overnight in Florence.

Day 2:

This morning after a really nice breakfast enjoy a fantastic private walking tour with our selected tour guide. Your guide will help you beat the crowds for personalized, stress-free visits to the "must-sees" and will also take you off the beaten track to show you some of the lesser-known corners and hidden gems of this magnificent city. The tour includes the escorted visit at the Accademia Gallery. Our experienced selected tour guides will help you to discover one of most important museums in the world. Our guides will take you back in time, on an animated journey through the history and art of Florence, in order to reveal secrets and tales linked to the artworks of the greatest Renaissance artists. Tonight dinner in Florence in one of the best restaurant of the city with a fantastic view of the town … Overnight in Florence.

Day 3:

Today departures to the stunning Cinque Terre area to your beautiful hotel nestled in the majestic cliffs overlooking Monterosso al Mare, the northernmost town of Cinque Terre on the gorgeous Ligurian coast. From its many jutting terraces and cobblestone patios, guests are invited to take in stunning panoramas of the sea, lose themselves in the fragrant perfumes of the Mediterranean flora, and bask in the warm rays of the sun. Tonight dinner from the terrace of your hotel where you will enjoy this view! Overnight in the Five lands.

Day 4:

Today a memorable experience with one of our best tour leaders, you will hike a cliff side trail which connects the Cinque Terre villages, and which provides some stunning views over the Ligurian Sea. The path is well-marked so you can walk at your own pace and take your time exploring the villages. Indeed, if you've had enough of hiking and find yourself happily lost in a sunny piazza overlooking the sea … Your selected local guide will share with you the best views of the five towns, which are a set of ancient seaside towns suspended high above the rocky cliffs overlooking the Mediterranean Sea. Overnight in the Five lands.

Day 5:

Today departure to Tuscany where you will discover the most beautiful area of this enchanting region. Your will drive you to your beautiful Hotel in Tuscany with a view of gentle hills rise and fall in a curvy sea of emerald trees and umber soil, occasionally broken up by a golden field, a silvery olive grove or the ancient cream of a stone Pieve. Today you may want to relax in the garden of your hotel or you may want to explore the area independently. Tonight welcome dinner in the restaurant of your hotel. Overnight in Tuscany. ( Volterra area)

Day 6:

This morning you will experience a beautiful walking tour with one of best local tour guides who will make Volterra's traditions and history come alive. It will be very exciting to discover an Alabaster workshop and tour a pair of great museums: the Guarnacci Etruscan Museum, one of Italy's best places to learn about this mysterious pre-Roman civilization; and the Pinocoteca, home to Rosso Fiorentino's from the Cross and other Renaissance works. After the tour you will have the rest of day to explore the city. Tonight nice dinner in a family local restaurant with a great view of the city. Overnight in Tuscany. ( Volterra area)

Day 7:

Today you may wish to discover the highlight of the Tuscany region through the hills of Chianti passing vineyards, villas and olive groves. The Chianti wine country is exceptionally scenic and pretty with fairy tale castles set amid rolling hills covered in vineyards, olive groves and cypress trees. Located just south of Florence, around the hamlets of Radda, Castellina and Gaiole, this is truly one of the most picturesque regions in Italy. You’ll visit top producers of the best Wine Chianti and attend an unforgettable wine tasting and a typical Italian lunch that we always suggest for food and wine lovers. In the afternoon stop in one of the nicest town in Tuscany Siena set the heart of Tuscany, this hilly town has maintained its medieval structure unaltered; its narrow streets, squares and aristocratic palaces are rich in history. I promises that here you will find beautiful nature, delicious food and wine and all of this accompanied with traditional Tuscan hospitality. Return to your hotel late afternoon. Overnight in Tuscany. ( Volterra area)

Day 8:

Today you will leave your hotel in Tuscany and drive back to Florence where you will drop off your car and get a first class train to Rome. At your arrival in Rome our lovely local driver will pick you up and drive you to your beautiful hotel located in one of the most enchanting & elegant areas of Rome, right in the heart of the historic centre of Rome, it is just a short walk from Piazza Navona, Campo dei Fiori, Castel Sant’Angelo, St. Peter’s Square, the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel. The hotel offers also a fantastic restaurant with a beautiful terrace overlooking the romantic eternal city. Tonight a welcome dinner in a typical Italian restaurant, located close to your hotel, where usually Italians enjoy a nice meal. Overnight in Rome.

Day 9:

Today experience a memorable private tour of Ancient Rome with one of our best tour guides. Your private guide will pick you up at the Hotel and with your private driver you will reach the Imperial Roman Forum where great men like Caesar, Augustus and Nero once walked the streets. You will see the Arch of Titus and House and Temple of the Vestal Virgins before entering with special VIP (skip the line) entrance tickets the Colosseum. In the ancient stadium your guide will explain everything from seating arrangements, the many games which were held, to where the losers we dragged out. The tour will conclude on the Palatine Hill; once the Beverly Hills of Rome. After the tour you may want to relax on one of the best spa in Rome or exploring the “ Bella Roma” Tonight choose your restaurant based on the list of our collection of restaurants in Rome. Overnight in Rome.

Day 10:

Today experience a fascinating tour for discovering the art and history of one of the most important museums in the world, The Vatican Museum. The tour will offer you an unforgettable experience with special reservations and skip the line priority entrance. Imagine yourself in the Sistine Chapel - almost alone. It is always regarded as "one of the best experiences in Rome". The tour includes also a visit of the St. Peter's Basilica … The rest of the afternoon is yours to savor la dolce vita… Tonight dinner wine tasting & dinner in one of the best restaurant in Rome. Overnight in Rome.

Day 11:

Today discover the area of Trastevere in Rome that is a picturesque medieval area located on the west bank of the Tiber. The area escaped the grand developments which changed the face of central Rome, and is a charming place to wander, full of really nice restaurant & nice store or you may want to discover the area that is full of food market located in Campo dei Fiori that is located two minutes from your hotel or the magical Piazza Navona, place of spectacle and still today a spectacular open air show; an architectural miracle in the heart of the Eternal City, filled with masterpieces in perfect harmony with each other enjoy the bars & nice restaurants located in this beautiful part of the town. Overnight in Rome.

Day 12:

Today after a nice breakfast private transfer from your hotel in Rome to the international airport of Rome.
