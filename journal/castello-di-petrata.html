Castello di Petrata

Umbria10 October 2025

Why it’s special

Above Assisi, Castello di Petrata is a 14th-century castle set among olive groves, orchards and woods. For couples dreaming of a Castello di Petrata wedding, the estate offers a romantic setting where historic architecture meets the natural beauty of Umbria. Gardens, courtyards and a panoramic pool look across the valley, while stone façades and ivy-clad walls create a cinematic backdrop for the celebration. The atmosphere is family-run, historic and effortlessly relaxed.

Ideal for

A multi-day, house-party wedding. Castello di Petrata offers accommodation for up to 100 guests, combining 22 hotel rooms for up to 60 people with 20 charming glamping tents accommodating another 40 guests within the estate. With the possibility of hosting wedding celebrations for up to 300 guests outdoors, the venue is ideal for couples dreaming of a destination wedding surrounded by family and friends. For larger groups, we can also arrange additional accommodation nearby, keeping the entire celebration centred around the castle.

Wedding experience Hold your ceremony in the dedicated garden with Assisi on the horizon, or choose a church rite in town and return for cocktails at sunset. Then it’s a long-table dinner in the courtyard under lights—the castle walls framing speeches and first dance. If the forecast shifts, we pair an elegant indoor plan so the mood stays seamless. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and layout

Ceremony: Dedicated ceremony garden with panoramic views of the Umbrian countryside and Assisi.

Aperitivo: Poolside area or panoramic terraces, ideal for sunset cocktails.

Dinner and reception: The castle’s outdoor spaces can accommodate wedding celebrations for up to 300 guests, while indoor spaces can host up to 250 guests.

Accommodation: The estate can accommodate up to 100 overnight guests, with 60 guests in 22 hotel rooms and an additional 40 guests in 20 glamping tents.

Our team carefully plans the layout, guest flow and event logistics according to the size and style of each celebration, ensuring a seamless experience throughout the wedding.

Good to know Menus lean local—welcome cocktail, abundant antipasti on the terrace, then plated courses and late-night party bites. The estate sits a short drive from Assisi, so portraits and church options are easy. Between events, guests can swim, wander the grounds or join relaxed tastings. We manage rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and production end to end, so the weekend stays calm and well-paced.
