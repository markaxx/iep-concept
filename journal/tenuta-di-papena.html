Tenuta di Papena

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the hills near Chiusdino, Tenuta di Papena is a rustic stone hamlet with a wide courtyard, wheat fields and a panoramic infinity pool. Spaces flow easily from the piazza to the pool gardens and the arcaded indoor rooms, so every moment stays close to the views. It feels honest and Tuscan, yet designed for celebrations

Ideal for: A multi-day destination wedding with your inner circle on site. The estate offers six self-catering apartments sleeping around 20–24 guests, depending on configuration. Larger groups stay nearby while we keep the house-party rhythm at the borgo. As a planning benchmark, receptions work best up to about 100 guests.

Wedding experience: Hold a symbolic ceremony in the gardens or among the grain fields. For a legal civil rite, the roofless Abbey of San Galgano sits about 5–6 km away; for a Catholic mass, the Chapel of Montesiepi is close by. After vows, move to golden-hour cocktails by the pool and dinner in the village square, with the arcaded rooms as a simple indoor fallback. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the flow stays seamless.

Good to know: Accessibility is thoughtful: one apartment is wheelchair-accessible and the outdoor areas are step-friendly. Proximity to San Galgano (about 5–6 km) keeps logistics smooth for town-hall ceremonies, while a three-night exclusive stay is a sensible baseline for weekend formats. Meanwhile, the pool and courtyard photograph beautifully day and night. We manage room blocks, transfers and timelines end-to-end.

Accommodation: 20 adults + 4 children

Max wedding guests: up to 100
