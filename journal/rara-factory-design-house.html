Rara Factory Design House

Umbria10 October 2025

Why it’s special On the hills outside Orvieto, Rara Factory is a design-forward country house surrounded by olives and fields. Industrial lines meet warm natural textures. Terraces, gardens and a saltwater pool keep every moment close to the view. The mood is intimate, stylish and quietly cinematic.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. Rooms and suites cluster around the main house, so your inner circle stays together while overflow lodges nearby. Distances are simple via Orvieto and the A1, which keeps transfers short and timing smooth. We coordinate rooming, transport and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic or civil rite on the lawn at golden hour. Then move to cocktails by the pool and a long-table dinner under lights. If the forecast shifts, linked interiors and the glassy living floor give you an elegant Plan B without losing atmosphere. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers—so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Ceremony: view lawn among olives. Aperitivo: pool deck facing the valley. Dinner: courtyard terrace for alfresco layouts; interiors as a refined fallback. As planning benchmarks, the property is ideal for intimate and mid-size celebrations; we right-size floor plans to guest count and season, keeping moves short and sightlines clean for photos.

Good to know The pool is saltwater and oriented to Orvieto’s skyline. On-site experiences include chef-hosted breakfasts, walks in the estate park and wellness add-ons. You’re a short hop from Orvieto’s town hall and churches for legal or religious rites; we handle paperwork, timings and production throughout.

Accommodation: The property has 3 rooms, for up to 6 guests

Max wedding guests: Maximum 25/ 35 wedding guests
