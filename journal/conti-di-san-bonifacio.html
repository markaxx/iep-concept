Conti di San Bonifacio

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the Maremma hills near Gavorrano, Conti di San Bonifacio crowns a ridge of vineyards and olives. The setting is cinematic yet calm. Stone architecture, terraces and an infinity pool frame long valley views. The resort blends a working winery with a boutique-luxury feel, so the experience stays rooted in place.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination wedding with a house-party rhythm. On site there are seven suites for about fourteen guests, with up to six extra beds if needed. Larger groups stay nearby while keeping the core celebration on property. Travel remains simple: Pisa is about 1 hour 20 minutes, and Florence roughly 1 hour 50 minutes.

Wedding experience: Say your vows among vines or in the ancient olive grove. Legal civil ceremonies are licensed on site, which keeps everything seamless. After golden-hour cocktails, dine under the lights on the lawn and dance beneath the trees. If you prefer, we curate a marquee for a grand alfresco look with full weather cover.

Good to know: Outdoors you can host up to around 130–150 guests; indoors the main common areas cover approximately 50–80, depending on layout. Exclusive-use formats typically span three nights, ideal for a welcome dinner, a relaxed pool day and a farewell brunch. We coordinate catering, bar, lighting, music and all suppliers, so the style stays consistent from first toast to last dance.

Accommodation: 7 luxury suites for 14 people. Extra beds can be added in each suite for a total of 6 extra people.

Max wedding guests: Ideal for 120 guests max
