Castello di Segalari

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: perched above the vineyards of Bolgheri and the village of Castagneto Carducci, Castello di Segalari is a 14th-century castle with sweeping views from the Maremma hills to the sea. Historic stone terraces, an elegant garden and a panoramic pool set the scene for sunset cocktails and alfresco dinners that feel cinematic yet relaxed. There’s also a private chapel within the grounds, adding a refined, intimate touch to the setting

Ideal for: couples planning a multi-day destination celebration with their inner circle staying on site. The castle offers eight double rooms for up to 16 guests, with the option to add two sofa beds on request, and it’s within easy reach of Pisa airport. For the reception, the two outdoor terraces suit seated dinners up to about 150 guests and the enchanted garden up to around 180; larger guest lists can be managed with a bespoke marquee solution.

Wedding experience: on-site Christian legal ceremonies can be arranged, while a Catholic church just a few kilometres away welcomes religious rites. The flow works beautifully outdoors—from poolside welcome drinks to a garden dinner—ending with cake cutting by the tower and a late-night party under the stars. Supplier choices are flexible, with trusted caterers available and space for fireworks or other special touches to make the weekend uniquely yours.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 16 people in 8 rooms (2 sofa beds can be added on request)

Max wedding guests: Up to 180 guests
