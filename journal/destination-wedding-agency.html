Destination Wedding Planners in Italy

Tuscany25 December 2018

Italian Wedding Event Planners is a destination wedding planning agency specializing in bespoke weddings and multi-day celebrations throughout Italy.

Founded in 2009, IEP combines the creative vision of Serena Ranieri and Ilaria Guarino with extensive local expertise and a highly personalized approach. Over the years, our team has planned hundreds of weddings for international couples, creating celebrations that reflect their individual stories, personalities and aspirations.

From Lake Como and the Amalfi Coast to Tuscany, Venice, Rome and Puglia, we take care of every detail, from venue scouting and wedding design to logistics and unforgettable guest experiences.

Your Destination Wedding Planners in Italy

Planning a wedding abroad requires much more than choosing a beautiful location. It means understanding local traditions, coordinating trusted professionals, managing logistics and creating a seamless experience for couples and their guests.

As experienced destination wedding planners in Italy, we guide our couples through every stage of the planning journey, offering professional advice, creative solutions and personal attention.

Whether you envision an intimate ceremony overlooking Lake Como, a romantic celebration on the Amalfi Coast, an elegant wedding in Tuscany or a spectacular multi-day event in Venice, our approach is always tailored to your vision, preferences and priorities.
