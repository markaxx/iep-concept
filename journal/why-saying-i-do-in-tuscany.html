Why saying I DO! In Tuscany

Tuscany27 March 2017

The Wedding Day is one of the most important days in one’s life.

From the moment the special emotion of the engagement arrives, everything around you changes, and you start thinking of it: THE BIG DAY.

It’s no wonder that you wish having it beautifully and perfectly organized!

Where do you have to start from? The first step is choosing the perfect backdrop for your special date. A wedding abroad, in a charming place, will guarantee unforgettable moments for the two of you, your families and friends.

Out of all the wonderful places on Earth, what’s the best where to go and exchange your vows?

We don’t have any doubts: with all due respect for every other place on the planet, it’s Italy...and TUSCANY above all Italian destinations!

Why should you have your wedding in Tuscany?

Here are the reasons to choose it as perfect place for your destination wedding!

Wonderful locations

Everywhere you look in Tuscany, breathtaking sceneries, classic art and magnificent architecture can be seen.

Do you dream of a city of art? Florence, first of all, stunning cradle of Renaissance, is an amazing city where history and art mesh in a lovely way with modern comforts, making the city a romantic and mind-filling place for you and your guests.

Tuscany is full of art and culture: besides Florence, other cities of art can welcome you and your guests with a charming atmosphere: Pisa, Siena, Lucca and more…you have plenty of choices!

If you think of a countryside wedding, Chianti region is perfect, with its wonderful landscapes, olives, vineyards, wine cellars…

For a wedding on the sea-side, the gorgeous Tuscan coast with its blue sea, or Elba Island, with its peculiar landscapes and pure crystalline sea could be the right place!

If you love the romantic atmosphere of a lake, Tuscany offers you the lakeshore at Torre del Lago Puccini, land of art and music, where most famous Opera composer Giacomo Puccini was born...

Excellent food

Italian food will conquer you and your guests! Tuscan food and wines will be the special touch to your marriage, leaving to your guests a fantastic memory of the time spent with you on your wedding date. A big choice of appetizers with local specialties, first courses with pasta above all, second courses based on meat, fish or vegetarian plates if you prefer, or - why not? – pizza!…not to speak about cakes and sparkling wines!

All these can be part of your wedding dinner, making it absolutely unforgettable.

Good weather

Italy is the land of sun and it generally has a good weather. Summers are dry and hot, winters can be clear and cold. These conditions are perfect for great wedding pictures and celebrations. During the warmer months in Tuscany you can have outdoor celebrations and parties with almost no chance of bad weather; in winter you can have incredible photos with a bright, blue sky and a cozy reception with candlelight.

Stunning pictures

A wedding in Tuscany allows memorable photos. Either your wedding ceremony will be held in an ancient villa, in the countryside or on the sea, the light is always remarkable, no matter the location!

You’ll have fantastic photos to remember your wedding date in Tuscany!

Art, Culture and Tradition

No doubt Tuscany is the right place if you love art and culture: worldwide known Museums like Uffizi, famous places of art like The Old Bridge or Pitti Palace, gorgeous Italian gardens like Boboli Gardens are here, ready to receive you! You can have dedicated guided tours with official expert tourist guides.

Moreover, our culture and traditions will be part of your wedding as well: in Italy there’s an important tradition of wedding favours called “bomboniere”: a small, decorated package containing three or five sugared almonds, called “confetti”, usually accompanied with a small gift: the “confetti” symbolize health, wealth, fertility, happiness, and long life. You can’t miss it! It will give a sense of authenticity to your guests, while they’re in a foreign land.

Relaxing time

Don’t forget to leave some time for you and your guests for some relaxing time during your stay in Tuscany! The region allows many possibilities, from guided tours to most famous Museums or cities of Art, to walks in the countryside, guided tours to wine cellars in the Chianti region, bike or vespa tours…or simply relaxing time at a SPA.

If you like it, you can also have an experience with a personal shopper, who will guide you to the best places where to buy Italian fashion!

Whatever you choose, it will be a true experience for you and your guests.

Well, above are just some of the reasons to choose Tuscany as wedding destination…come and celebrate your wedding immersed in the beauties of this region, its art, culture and traditions!

We’re waiting for you…!!
