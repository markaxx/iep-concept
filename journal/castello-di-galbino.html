Castello di Galbino

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special In the hills near Anghiari and Arezzo, Castello di Galbino is a 16th-century castle with four round towers and open countryside views. Stone loggias, lawns and an infinity pool create an easy indoor–outdoor flow. The mood is historic and private, yet relaxed for a weekend celebration.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding with your core group on site. The castle lists 10 bedrooms for about 20 overnight guests, which keeps everyone close. Additional guests sleep nearby while we center the events at Galbino. Travel works well via Florence or Perugia.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic or civil ceremony in the gardens or beside the loggias. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. If needed, we pivot to indoor salons without losing atmosphere. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout As planning benchmarks, weddings scale up to about 120 guests outdoors. Indoors, linked rooms suit smaller seated plans and late-night dancing. We place ceremony, aperitivo, dinner and cake in distinct zones to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The pool sits below the main façade with wide views for portraits. The castle’s plan is compact, so transitions are short and guest flow stays smooth. For overnights beyond the 20 in-castle beds, we arrange nearby lodging and transfers. We manage timelines, paperwork and production end to end.

Accommodation: The castle has 12 bedrooms and can accommodate up to 24 guests on site

Max wedding guests: Up to 100 guests
