wedding entertainment ideas

8 April 2013

Wedding Entertaintement is not just about music! You are wrong if you think so! You must chose the right way to entertain your guests to make them remeber your special day for long time...

For example, a photo booth would be a lovely idea to entertain people of any age. You could place a basket with wigs & hats, feather boas and o bjects for dressing up. Even the shyest will be involved in the game!

Or you could create a real photo shoots corner, and by hiring a professional photographer dedicated to take funny pictures of you and your guests, you will get some unforgettable memories, it's guaranteed!

Are you planning an outdoor wedding during the summer? Do you have a beautiful garden and do you really want to have fun? if it so then, you should definitely organize a croquet challenge, a game of boules ( married vs bachelors), or a giant Jenga!

Better yet, how about a treasure hunt?:-)
