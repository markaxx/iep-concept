Villa di Ulignano

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: Villa di Ulignano sits on a hilltop between Volterra and San Gimignano, a 17th-century manor framed by centuries-old trees and sweeping Tuscan views across a 17-acre estate. Commissioned by Jacopo Inghirami and designed by Florentine architect Gherardo Silvani, the villa later became a beloved retreat for Italian cinema under producer Franco Cristaldi, whose circle included Luchino Visconti. The result is a historic home with creative soul, perfect for house-party weddings that feel both elegant and relaxed.

Ideal for: couples planning a multi-day destination celebration with their closest guests staying on site. The villa and its adjacent buildings offer an ideal capacity of about 30 beds, expandable to 37 using daybeds and up to 40 with additional beds; larger guest lists are simple to accommodate in nearby lodgings. Events are recommended for 20–80 and can comfortably scale to around 120 with off-site accommodations. The property is rented exclusively, with minimum stays of three nights or one week depending on season, and weeks typically run Tuesday to Tuesday.

Wedding experience: ceremonies and dinners unfold in the gardens, by the panoramic pool, or in the villa’s charming courtyard, with a private chapel for quiet moments or blessings. For receptions, there’s a large indoor room for seated or buffet service, and an outstanding set of amenities for a full wedding weekend—20×10 m pool area, Finnish sauna and gym, a projection room, games spaces and a fully equipped professional kitchen. Between events, guests can enjoy cooking classes, wine tastings, truffle hunts, e-bike rides or even a hot-air balloon flight for a quintessential Tuscan escape.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 30 guests across 15 bedrooms with capacity to extend to up to 40 guests with additional beds

Max wedding guests: Maximum 120 wedding guests
