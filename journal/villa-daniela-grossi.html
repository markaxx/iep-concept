Villa Daniela Grossi

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: On the gentle hills outside Lucca, Villa Daniela Grossi is a 17th-century residence with a sculpted Italian garden and long country views. The mood is elegant and calm. Frescoed salons open to lawns, hedged allées and a heated panoramic pool, so every moment stays close to the landscape.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination wedding with a house-party rhythm. As planning benchmarks, independent guides note weddings up to about 80 guests, with roughly 16 bedrooms on site for around 40 people. If you need more beds, sister property Villa La Volpe sits a short walk away and adds capacity. We coordinate room blocks and guest logistics so everyone feels at home.

Wedding experience: Civil ceremonies are licensed at the villa through the Comune di Capannori’s “Villa Weddings” program, which keeps the day seamless. Then the flow is simple: vows in the Italian garden, aperitivo among the hedges, dinner in the Lemon House or under the trees, and dancing after dark. Multiple indoor options give you a refined all-weather plan. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, matching your brief step by step.

Good to know: Spaces include the villa salons, the charming Limonaia and broad terraces beside the gardens and pool. The setting photographs beautifully at golden hour and works in every season thanks to the indoor rooms. We can also fold Villa La Volpe into the plan for larger overnights while keeping the celebration centered at Daniela Grossi.

Accommodation: 16 bedrooms that can accommodate up to 40 guests. Villa is rented for exclusive use

Max wedding guests: Up to 80 guests
