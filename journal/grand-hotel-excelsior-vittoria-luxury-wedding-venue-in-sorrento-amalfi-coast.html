Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria – Luxury Wedding Venue in Sorrento, Amalfi Coast

Amalfi Coast2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria is one of the most luxurious wedding venues in Sorrento, offering an unparalleled combination of historical charm and modern elegance. Perched on a cliff with panoramic views of the Bay of Naples and the iconic Mount Vesuvius, this five-star hotel is renowned for its timeless beauty, lush gardens, and exceptional service. With its blend of old-world charm and luxurious amenities, it is an ideal destination for couples seeking an unforgettable luxury wedding in Sorrento.

Ideal for: Couples seeking a glamorous, sophisticated wedding in Sorrento, with breathtaking views of the Bay of Naples and Mount Vesuvius. The Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria is perfect for those who want a luxury wedding with a mix of historic charm, panoramic vistas, and impeccable service in one of the most prestigious wedding venues in Italy.

Wedding Experience: Exchange vows in the hotel’s lush gardens, with the stunning backdrop of the Bay of Naples and Mount Vesuvius. The breathtaking views and romantic atmosphere create a perfect setting for an unforgettable ceremony. After the vows, enjoy a reception on the hotel’s panoramic terraces, where guests can dine while overlooking the sparkling bay. Indulge in traditional Neapolitan cuisine, paired with fine wines from the region. For an additional intimate touch, the hotel offers a private Limoncello garden, a secluded space perfect for a romantic wedding celebration.

With its luxury accommodations, exceptional service, and picturesque views, Grand Hotel Excelsior Vittoria offers couples a charming and elegant wedding experience in the heart of Sorrento.
