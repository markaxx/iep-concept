La Torre sulla via Francigena

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: set along the historic Via Francigena between San Gimignano and Volterra, La Torre is an adults-only organic farmstay wrapped in six hectares of vineyards, olive trees and gardens. The feel is that of a private Tuscan home with a contemporary, design-forward heart, complete with panoramic pool and jacuzzi and wide-open countryside views.

Ideal for: couples dreaming of an intimate, multi-day destination wedding with everyone under one roof. There are eight uniquely designed rooms inspired by Tuscan wildlife, sleeping 16 guests and extendable to 19 with extra beds; additional accommodation is easy to arrange nearby. Weddings are typically hosted from June to September, when long, golden evenings make alfresco celebrations effortless. Florence and Pisa airports sit about 45–55 minutes away, keeping travel simple

Wedding experience: a purpose-built ceremony lawn lies at the end of a rose-and-vine walkway, and the venue is licensed for legal civil ceremonies on site with an officiant from the local comune. Dinner flows naturally to a single long table at the vineyard’s edge for up to 120 guests, with tables, linens, fresh florals and lighting included; the pool terrace with pergola and band stand is perfect for cocktails and late-night dancing. Throughout the weekend you can add a wood-oven pizza night, a garden picnic with outdoor cinema and surround sound, or a relaxed poolside BBQ brunch.

Good to know: La Torre is adults only (+12). The pool area has a ramp for easy access, and the team offers in-house catering with flexible supplier options if you prefer an external caterer. The vineyard long-table setup comfortably reaches 120 covers, while the gardens and terraces provide natural back-ups and break-out areas as needed.

Accommodation: 8 bedrooms each individually designed based on an animal of Tuscany. Total accommodation of 16. This can increase to 19 with extra beds.

Max wedding guests: Up to 120 guests
