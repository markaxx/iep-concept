Hotel Splendido – Luxury Wedding Venue in Portofino, Liguria: Glamorous Seaside Weddings with Panoramic Views

Liguria2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Hotel Splendido, located in the picturesque seaside village of Portofino on the Ligurian coast, is a luxury wedding venue renowned for its exclusive service, breathtaking panoramic views of the Ligurian Sea, and lush, beautifully landscaped gardens. This iconic hotel offers a glamorous and romantic atmosphere, making it the perfect destination for couples seeking an elegant seaside wedding in one of Italy’s most sought-after locations.

Ideal for: Couples looking for a luxurious wedding with panoramic views of the Ligurian Sea. Hotel Splendido is perfect for those who dream of a glamorous wedding celebration in a charming coastal village, with impeccable service, exquisite settings, and world-class dining.

Wedding Experience: Exchange vows on the hotel’s stunning terrace, where the Ligurian Sea serves as a magnificent backdrop for your ceremony. Afterward, enjoy an intimate reception at the hotel’s elegant restaurant, offering guests fresh, flavorful Ligurian seafood and locally inspired dishes. With its combination of exclusive luxury, romantic coastal views, and exceptional service, Hotel Splendido ensures a memorable and sophisticated wedding experience in Portofino.
