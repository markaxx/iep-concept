italian style wedding favors!

5 April 2013

The bomboniera it’s a small gift we want to donate to those who have attended the wedding, as a memory of a special day a special experience.

In Italy is really expected from the guests, is part of the tradition. It can be anything you want, made out ceramic, fabric, glass, crystal, wood…

also according to tradition the gift should be accompanied by sugared almonds, wrapped in tissue and tied to the favor itself.

For your Italian wedding you could also opt for a different kind of wedding favor such as a gastronomic one, and you can easily understand why. There is a big campaign in Italy about eating well and mostly to bring back in our culture, the Italian eating style. It is nice to give as a gift a particular product of the region you are having the wedding.

Truffle sauce put in a nice glass tin wrapped with a ribbon or packed in a nice box with the initials or the name of the couple, or a fine extra virgin olive oil bottle or limoncello if you are getting married in the Sorrento and Amalfi area. Everywhere you decide to marry you will find the perfect gastronomic gifts for your guests.
