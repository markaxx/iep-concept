Borgo Laticastelli

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special On a ridge near Rapolano Terme, Laticastelli is a medieval hamlet turned country relais with wide views over the Crete Senesi. Terraces, gardens and an infinity pool create an easy indoor–outdoor flow, while stone lanes and courtyards keep the setting intimate and cinematic. You’re about 20 km from Siena, so guest logistics stay simple.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. On site there are 32 rooms—sleeping up to about 80 guests—so your inner circle can stay together in the borgo. Additional beds are easy to arrange nearby for larger groups. We coordinate room blocks and transfers end to end.

Wedding experience Host a civil or symbolic ceremony on the lawns or by the pool with the hills behind you. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and dinner on the Brunello Terrace or in the Fountain Garden. If the forecast shifts, the in-house restaurant offers a refined Plan B with two halls and a covered terrace. We curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Outdoors, receptions work beautifully up to roughly 100–120 guests depending on the terrace or garden used. Indoors, the restaurant seats about 100 and links directly to covered space for a seamless fallback. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The borgo layout gives you distinct zones for ceremony, aperitivo, dinner and dancing without long transfers. On site, guests enjoy the panoramic infinity pool and the surrounding countryside. Nearby hotels cover overflow—up to around 120 more beds—while we keep the core celebration inside the hamlet.

Accommodation: 33 rooms sleeping up to 80 guests

Max wedding guests: Perfect for up to 100 guests
