From an intimate countryside celebration to an elegant seaside wedding or a multi-day experience surrounded by family and friends, Puglia combines Italian traditions, exceptional cuisine and unforgettable scenery.

Top Reasons to Choose Puglia for Your Wedding

Historic Masserias: The Essence of a Puglia Wedding

One of the most distinctive features of a destination wedding in Puglia is the opportunity to celebrate in a traditional masseria. These historic countryside estates, often surrounded by centuries-old olive trees, combine authentic Apulian architecture with refined hospitality and beautiful outdoor spaces.

From the elegant masserias of Savelletri and Fasano to the enchanting countryside of the Itria Valley, couples can enjoy romantic ceremonies, alfresco receptions and multi-day celebrations in an atmosphere that feels both intimate and extraordinary.

Some of our favourite wedding venues in Puglia include:

  • Masseria Torre Coccaro – A beautiful countryside estate surrounded by olive groves, offering an authentic and elegant atmosphere.
  • Masseria San Domenico – A distinctive luxury property near the Adriatic coast, combining traditional architecture with refined hospitality.
  • Masseria Torre Maizza – An elegant destination in Savelletri, ideal for couples seeking understated luxury and an authentic Apulian setting.

Many masserias also offer accommodation, making them particularly appealing for destination weddings where couples wish to spend quality time with their guests.

Alberobello: A Fairytale Wedding in the Land of Trulli

Alberobello, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is renowned for its iconic trulli: traditional limestone buildings with distinctive conical roofs.

These extraordinary structures create one of the most recognisable landscapes in southern Italy and provide a charming setting for couples seeking a wedding experience deeply connected to local heritage.

The surrounding Itria Valley offers beautiful countryside properties, traditional architecture and intimate venues where couples can celebrate surrounded by the timeless atmosphere of Puglia.

Ostuni: The White City of Romance

Known as the White City, Ostuni is one of Puglia’s most picturesque destinations.

Its whitewashed buildings, narrow streets, historic piazzas and panoramic views towards the Adriatic Sea create a wonderful setting for romantic celebrations and wedding photography.

The surrounding countryside is home to beautiful masserias and private estates, offering couples the opportunity to combine the charm of Ostuni with the privacy and elegance of a countryside wedding.

Lecce: Baroque Beauty for Your Wedding

Often called the Florence of the South, Lecce is celebrated for its extraordinary Baroque architecture, historic palaces and elegant squares.

For couples dreaming of a sophisticated celebration surrounded by art and history, Lecce offers a distinctive alternative to the region’s rural and coastal wedding venues.

Its beautiful architecture and vibrant atmosphere also make it an excellent destination for guests wishing to explore southern Puglia before or after the wedding.

Savelletri and Fasano: Luxury Weddings by the Adriatic Sea

The coastal area around Savelletri and Fasano has become one of Puglia’s most desirable destinations for luxury weddings.

Here, the Adriatic coastline meets ancient olive groves, historic estates and exceptional hospitality.

Couples looking for an elegant wedding weekend can discover extraordinary properties that combine contemporary luxury with traditional Apulian character.

Among the region’s most celebrated destinations is Borgo Egnazia, a remarkable property inspired by the architecture and atmosphere of a traditional Apulian village.

This area is particularly appealing for multi-day celebrations, allowing couples to organise welcome dinners, wedding receptions and farewell gatherings while offering their guests an unforgettable Italian experience.

Unique Experiences to Enhance Your Puglia Wedding

A destination wedding in Puglia is about much more than the wedding day itself.

The region offers wonderful opportunities to create a complete experience for your guests, combining culture, relaxation, gastronomy and local traditions.

Welcome Parties and Pre-Wedding Celebrations

A relaxed welcome dinner beneath the olive trees, a traditional Apulian evening with local food and music, or an elegant seaside gathering can be the perfect way to begin your wedding celebrations.

For couples hosting guests from different countries, these moments create an opportunity for everyone to connect before the wedding day.

Discovering Puglia Together

Some of the most memorable activities to include in a wedding itinerary are:

  • Grotte di Castellana: Explore an extraordinary underground network of caves and impressive natural formations.
  • Boat Excursions: Discover Puglia’s beautiful coastline, hidden coves and crystal-clear waters.
  • Wine and Olive Oil Tastings: Introduce your guests to the region’s agricultural traditions through visits to local vineyards and olive oil producers.
  • Exploring Historic Villages: Discover charming towns such as Alberobello, Locorotondo, Monopoli and Ostuni.
  • Traditional Cooking Experiences: Enjoy an authentic culinary experience and learn how to prepare some of Puglia’s most beloved dishes.

With thoughtful planning, these experiences can become part of a memorable wedding weekend rather than simply optional excursions.

Traditional Puglian Cuisine: A Feast for Your Wedding Guests

Food is an essential part of Italian wedding celebrations, and Puglia offers an extraordinary culinary heritage.

The region is known for its fresh local ingredients, exceptional olive oil, traditional recipes and generous hospitality.

Some of the most beloved Apulian specialities include:

  • Orecchiette con Cime di Rapa: Traditional handmade pasta served with turnip greens.
  • Focaccia Barese: Soft, flavourful focaccia traditionally topped with tomatoes and olives.
  • Burrata: A deliciously creamy fresh cheese originating from Puglia.
  • Fresh Seafood: An essential part of the culinary traditions of the Adriatic and Ionian coasts.
  • Pasticciotto: A traditional pastry filled with custard, particularly associated with Salento.
  • Primitivo and Negroamaro Wines: Two of the region’s best-known wine varieties.

Whether you envision an elegant plated dinner, an atmospheric outdoor reception or a traditional Apulian food experience, local cuisine can become one of the most memorable elements of your wedding.
