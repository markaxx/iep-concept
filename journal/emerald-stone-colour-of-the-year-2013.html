emerald stone - colour of the year 2013

9 April 2013

Emerald Green is the Pantone Colour of the Year for 2013 and and we are really hoping it will start to feature in this year’s weddings.

We love green, the color of the intelligence, we love its infinite variations and nuances...

But be careful: it is very easy to overdo it, and so easy to go wrong with the combinations. As the emerald is predominant and very distinctive, I highly recommend the light pink and white go with it. It will become stylish to die for.
