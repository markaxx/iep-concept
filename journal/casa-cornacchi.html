Casa Cornacchi

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: in the Val d’Ambra countryside between Siena and Arezzo, Casa Cornacchi is a rustic stone hamlet with wide valley views, an infinity pool and a sequence of terraces and gardens designed for alfresco moments. The covered Bellavista terrace gives you an all-weather dining space that still feels outdoors, so the sunset atmosphere never gets lost. It’s intimate and family-style, yet purpose-built for destination weddings

Ideal for: a multi-day, house-party celebration with your closest people staying on site. The estate sleeps up to about 60 guests across 25 bedrooms with 10 extra beds, and nearby partner villas take the total to around 93 if needed. Receptions work beautifully from 20 to 80 guests and can scale to about 120. Exclusive use is standard, with a short minimum stay to secure the whole borgo. Florence and Pisa are the natural gateway airports

Wedding experience: you can hold a legal civil ceremony on the property or opt for a symbolic or religious rite, then flow from an aperitivo by the pool to dinner on the Bellavista terrace and dancing under the stars. Signature group moments—pizza night, BBQ night, pasta night—and touches like an artisan gelato cart keep the weekend relaxed and joyful without leaving the estate

Good to know: seated events are possible up to roughly 120 guests with a minimum of about 40 adults; on-site beds reach around 60 and off-site shuttles are easily arranged. There’s an infinity pool and panoramic jacuzzi, music typically runs late within local limits

Accommodation: Sleeps 54 guests in total over 2 locations (15 mins apart)

Max wedding guests: 80 people is the perfect size for this venue but they can host up to 120
