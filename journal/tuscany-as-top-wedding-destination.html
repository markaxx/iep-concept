Tuscany as Top Wedding Destination

Tuscany20 February 2017

Tuscany is one of the the celebs’ favourite lands to celebrate their most important day… its appeal makes it the Top Wedding Destination in Italy!

Many world known celebs have already chosen Tuscany to celebrate their weddings, starting from David Bowie, most famous singer who, unexpectedly at that time, in 1992 chose Florence for his wedding date. Major sport celebs, like Wesley Sneijder, Dutch football champ who said yes in Castelnuovo Berardenga (Siena) and Joe Hart, goalkeeper of Manchester City, who chose Vincigliata Castle in Fiesole (Florence) are only an example. This Region counts, among its brides, Carolina Di Borbone, Dutch Queen’s niece, who married in San Miniato (Pisa) as well as Aradhana Lohia, daughter of the Indo-Thai tycoon Aloke Lohia, one of the richest men in the world, who married in Florence. Recently Florence has also been the perfect stage for the fabulous wedding between Roshni and Rohan Mehta, son of Indian millionaire Yogesh Mehta.

Tuscany Region, following this trend, is working to improve the reception of foreign couples involving all itsTown Halls and municipalities, so as to make necessary papers collection easier for applicants. In parallel the Region promotes Tuscany as Wedding Destination through various and important activities. Some among the main Tuscan Historical Residences are also organizing themselves into a network to improve their competitiveness as Wedding Locations and become real trend setters.

And, if Florence with its Luxury Hotels and Historical Palaces as well as Chianti area with its wine-cellars are already famous all over the world, there’s a reality not well known yet: the Tuscan Coast, in all its beauty. It extends with beaches, cliffs and ancient towers overlooking a sea officially recognized as one of the cleanest in Italy. From Forte dei Marmi in Versilia to the still wild land of Maremma or the crystalline purity of Tuscan Islands, the Tuscan Coast offers innumerable possibilities to organize unforgettable weddings and no doubt it will become a top high level Wedding Destination.
