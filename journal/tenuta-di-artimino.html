Tenuta di Artimino

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: On a hilltop west of Florence, Tenuta di Artimino centres on Villa Medicea La Ferdinanda, a UNESCO-listed Medici residence. Terraces, lawns and frescoed salons open to wide valley views. The estate feels grand yet easy: a historic villa, a wine resort and a medieval borgo all in one place.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination celebration with everyone close by. Your inner circle stays on site between the Paggeria Medicea hotel and the borgo apartments, with further beds in Le Fagianaie. Across the estate there are roughly 97–102 rooms and apartments, so guest logistics stay simple while the party keeps a house-party rhythm. Florence is about 20 km away.

Wedding experience: Civil ceremonies are licensed at La Ferdinanda through the Comune di Carmignano. You can marry on the villa lawns or choose a view terrace, then move into golden-hour cocktails and an alfresco dinner under lights. If the weather turns, the villa offers more than twenty indoor rooms and grand halls as refined back-ups, so the timeline stays smooth. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers to match your brief.

Good to know: The borgo sits a short walk below the villa. Hotel rooms cluster in the Paggeria; additional apartments line the village streets and Le Fagianaie. Pools, spa and winery experiences keep guests happily on property between events. As a planning benchmark, the estate can accommodate large groups across its lodging, while event spaces scale from intimate salons to broad terraces and gardens. We handle rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and church liaisons end to end.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 300 guests across Tenuta di Artimino Hotel and Apartments

Max wedding guests: 500
