Borgo Siliano

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special On the border between Umbria and Tuscany, Antico Podere Siliano (Borgo Siliano) is a family-run country estate dating to the late 1800s. Vineyards, olive groves and gardens roll out in every direction, with a panoramic pool and stone houses that open straight to the view. The setting feels authentic and calm—perfect for a slow, multi-day celebration.

Ideal for A house-party wedding with your inner circle staying on site. The borgo balances intimacy with scale: accommodations for about 45 guests and event capacity up to roughly 150. Between moments, the estate’s own wine and olive oil add a true sense of place, and we weave in relaxed experiences—pizza night, pasta night, vineyard dinner or a pool-day brunch—to keep the rhythm easy.

Wedding experience Say your vows on the lawn facing the cypress line, then ease into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. If weather shifts, we pivot layouts to keep everything seamless without losing the atmosphere. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays consistent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout As planning benchmarks: sleep about 45 on site; host up to around 150 for the main night outdoors. The grounds give you clear zones for ceremony, aperitivo and dinner, which reduces moves and protects sightlines for photos. Wet-weather plans pair indoor spaces with a marquee if needed. We right-size floor plans to your guest list and season.

Good to know Amenities make the weekend feel like a retreat: panoramic pool, jacuzzi area, tennis court, table tennis and e-bikes. Each apartment has a private patio and BBQ, while reserved umbrellas and loungers keep pool time effortless. The team offers gentle activities and local tours; we coordinate the schedule so your guests can join without fuss. Pets are welcome, and access across key outdoor areas is step-friendly.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 56 guests in the following: 6 apartments, 3 double rooms, 2 eco villas

Max wedding guests: up to 150 guests
