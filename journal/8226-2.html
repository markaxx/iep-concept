Podere Montale

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: Podere Montale is a 17th-century hamlet on a 200-hectare wine and olive estate on the slopes of Monte Amiata, with courtyards, gardens and a pool looking across the UNESCO Val d’Orcia. It feels like a tiny private village—stone arcades, a belvedere over the pool, and interiors that open straight to the views—so every moment flows indoors and out without losing the Tuscan scenery.

Ideal for: a multi-day destination wedding with your closest guests staying on site and everyone else nearby. The Borgo offers eighteen accommodations for forty-eight adults, extendable to fifty-seven, which keeps the house-party rhythm while giving you room to stretch out across lawns and terraces. Licensed civil ceremonies can be hosted on the property, so you can exchange vows with the valley as your backdrop and keep the whole celebration in one place.

Wedding experience: picture a ceremony on the lawn or under the arcades, a golden-hour aperitivo by the pool and dinner on the belvedere or a garden marquee, with late-night dancing in the hamlet’s sheltered spaces. We curate food and wine with the estate’s in-house restaurant and cellar, arranging menu design, tastings and service so the cuisine feels rooted in place. Between events we build a relaxed program—vineyard tastings, jeep rides through the property, walks to the private riverbank or day trips to Montalcino, Pienza and Siena—so the weekend becomes a shared holiday.

Good to know: the Borgo sits in Seggiano, Grosseto, about two hours from Florence and roughly ninety minutes from Siena, with Florence Amerigo Vespucci as the most convenient airport. Access is via a short private unpaved road, there’s parking for around thirty cars, and the hamlet includes lounges with fireplaces, a meeting room and a service kitchen to support logistics and wet-weather plans. We handle supplier management, styling, lighting and timelines end-to-end, keeping venue coordination off your plate.

Accommodation: The maximum capacity of the Borgo is 50 people. The maximum capacity of the Winery is 14 people
