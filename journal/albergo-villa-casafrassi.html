Albergo Villa Casafrassi

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In Chianti Classico near Castellina, Casafrassi is a historic hamlet wrapped in 100 hectares of vineyards and olive groves. An 18th-century villa anchors the estate. Beside it, a 13th-century cloister and English gardens set a romantic, old-world tone. There is also a petite church for intimate moments. A panoramic pool completes the picture.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination wedding with a house-party rhythm. Your core group stays on site, while additional guests lodge nearby. As planning benchmarks, independent guides list around 36 rooms and suites in rustic Tuscan style. Travel is simple between Siena and Florence. We coordinate rooming lists, transfers and schedules end to end.

Wedding experience: Hold a legal civil or symbolic ceremony on the grounds, or choose the small on-site church for a private blessing. Then move into golden-hour cocktails in the gardens and a long-table dinner under lights. Outdoors, capacities reach about 200; indoors, plan for roughly 150 across the villa’s salons and covered spaces. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Good to know: Spaces flow naturally between the cloister, lawns, terraces and the pool area—so transitions stay short and photo-friendly. The estate also produces Chianti Classico, extra-virgin olive oil and grappa, which adds a local touch to tastings and favors. Meanwhile, a small meeting room seats up to 25 for welcome briefings or beauty set-ups. We handle all timelines and on-site production.

Accommodation: The venue is composed of 3 buildings: the main villa, with 14 double rooms + 2 single rooms, “Foresteria” annex with 7 double rooms and “Il Chiostro” annex with 5 double rooms + 7 triple rooms

Max wedding guests: Up to 140 guests
