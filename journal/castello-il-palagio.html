Castello Il Palagio

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special In Chianti near Florence, Castello Il Palagio is a 13th-century castle with gardens “all’italiana,” a cloistered courtyard and a small consecrated chapel. Vineyards and olive groves surround the estate, and the arrival is scenic along a cypress driveway. The mood is historic yet effortless.

Ideal for A multi-day destination celebration with a house-party rhythm. The setting sits in San Casciano Val di Pesa, about 17 km from Florence, so travel is simple for guests. We coordinate rooming plans nearby while keeping the core moments inside the castle walls.

Wedding experience Exchange vows in the courtyard, the gardens or the petite chapel. Moreover, civil ceremonies are allowed on site, which keeps the timeline smooth. After golden-hour cocktails, move to dinner under the loggia or outdoors with the castle as your backdrop; indoor salons convert to a dance floor after dark. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and flow The main courtyard hosts up to about 180 guests. Indoors, the Hall of Arms seats around 70 (about 74 m²) and can pair with the Guard Room; auditorium-style layouts reach roughly 100. From April to October, a permanent outdoor marquee is typically available, adding flexible cover for larger dinners. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines.

Good to know Spaces include the courtyard, loggia, formal garden with fountain and several interior rooms across two levels. The castle sits between Florence and Siena in classic wine country, so pre- and post-events feel naturally local. We handle permits, paperwork and timelines end to end

Accommodation: None

Max wedding guests: 180 guests

Where to stay: Extensive list of local accommodation on request
