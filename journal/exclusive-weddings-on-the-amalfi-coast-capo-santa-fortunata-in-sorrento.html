Exclusive Weddings on the Amalfi Coast: Capo Santa Fortunata in Sorrento

Amalfi Coast2 December 2024

Capo Santa Fortunata is a breathtaking wedding venue on the Sorrento Peninsula, just minutes from Sorrento. Offering panoramic views of the Bay of Naples, this exclusive location blends rustic charm with Mediterranean beauty. The venue features lush gardens, elegant terraces, and intimate spaces, making it an ideal choice for couples seeking a romantic, peaceful, and scenic wedding experience.

Ideal for: Couples looking for a private, serene wedding venue with stunning views of the Amalfi Coast. Capo Santa Fortunata is perfect for those desiring a charming, rustic setting with easy access to both the vibrant town of Sorrento and the tranquil coast.

Experience: Exchange vows in one of Capo Santa Fortunata’s lush private gardens or on the terrace overlooking the glittering Mediterranean Sea. The reception can take place in the venue’s outdoor dining area or inside one of its elegant banquet rooms, surrounded by rich greenery. Guests will indulge in exquisite Sorrentine cuisine, while enjoying breathtaking views of the Bay of Naples. The venue’s proximity to Sorrento makes it convenient for guest accommodations, providing the perfect blend of seclusion and accessibility for a memorable wedding.
