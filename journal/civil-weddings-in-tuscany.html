Civil weddings in Tuscany

Tuscany10 May 2017

Civil weddings in Italy are legally binding marriages which usually take place in our beautiful local Town Halls.

Recently in Tuscany many venues have been recognized and approved by Tuscan authorities as Town Halls, where the two things, ceremony and reception, can be organized in the same place.

These venues are Villas, Castles, historical Palaces and Gardens: wonderful settings for your Legal civil wedding ceremony in Tuscany!

This formula is more and more requested by couples who choose our beautiful Region as wedding destination, as it’s not only a practical matter, but also one that allows many beautiful and original solutions for the organization of the wedding day...

Of course there are special Town Halls that are incomparable for their beauty and never lose their charm, notwithstanding the new trend!

The “Sala Rossa” in Palazzo Vecchio in Florence is the main Florence Town Hall and its magic atmosphere represents an irrestistible attraction for the couples who go for a wedding in the city of Florence.

Whatever you decide for your civil wedding ceremony in Tuscany, it will be a magic moment! We will assist you with all legal documents and necessary paperwork.
