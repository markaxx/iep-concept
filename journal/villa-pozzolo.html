Villa Pozzolo

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the Montaione countryside between Florence and Siena, Villa Pozzolo feels like a private Tuscan hamlet. Vine-clad facades, courtyards and an 18 m saltwater pool open to rolling hills. There is even a petite chapel for intimate moments. The estate blends bohemian character with practical event spaces, so the day flows easily.

Ideal for: A multi-day, house-party celebration. Exclusive use is standard, with a typical three-night stay. On site the mix of suites and apartments sleeps about 50, which keeps your core group together while others lodge nearby. Travel is straightforward from Florence and Pisa.

Wedding experience: Say “I do” in the chapel or outside among the lawns. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. Indoors, the stone barn and salons seat up to around 90; outdoors, courtyards and gardens scale to roughly 150. The Winter Garden (about 70 m²) and the dedicated dance floor support a lively after-party for roughly 120 friends—without losing the villa feel. We curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, end to end.

Good to know: Guides note “all-inclusive” stays with meals and drinks bundled, plus no formal music curfew—useful for late dancing in season. On the ground, you’ll find 40 hectares of grounds, a wine cellar, small gym, library and creative rooms for activities. We plan tastings, yoga, cooking classes and gentle countryside outings around your timetable.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 50 people in 24 rooms and suites

Max wedding guests: Recommended for 80-100 guests, host up to 120 guests
