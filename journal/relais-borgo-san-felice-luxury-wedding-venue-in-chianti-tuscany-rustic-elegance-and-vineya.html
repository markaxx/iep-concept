Relais Borgo San Felice – Luxury Wedding Venue in Chianti, Tuscany: Rustic Elegance and Vineyard Views

Tuscany2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Nestled in the heart of Chianti, Tuscany, Relais Borgo San Felice is a luxurious resort set within a historic village surrounded by vineyards and olive groves. With its stunning architecture, panoramic vineyard views, and exceptional service, this charming estate offers an authentic yet refined atmosphere, making it an ideal destination for a Tuscan wedding. The unique blend of natural beauty and historic charm makes Relais Borgo San Felice a dream location for couples seeking a romantic wedding in Tuscany.

Ideal for: Couples who desire a rustic yet elegant wedding in the heart of the Tuscan wine country. This venue is perfect for wine lovers and those who wish to embrace the relaxed, romantic pace of life in Tuscany. Relais Borgo San Felice provides an exceptional setting for couples seeking an authentic Tuscan wedding experience surrounded by breathtaking landscapes.

Wedding Experience: Exchange vows in the resort’s lush gardens, or opt for a romantic outdoor ceremony amidst the vineyards. Afterward, celebrate with a traditional Tuscan wedding feast, featuring local, seasonal ingredients and wine pairings from the estate’s own vineyard. Whether you choose to dine inside the elegant estate hall or enjoy dinner under the stars with views of the vineyard-dotted hills, your reception at Relais Borgo San Felice promises to be an unforgettable celebration of love and Tuscany’s rich heritage.
