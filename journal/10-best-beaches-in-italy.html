10 best beaches in italy!

Sardinia14 May 2013

Italy is one of the best seaside wedding destinations; with so many beaches it may be hard to pick one. After all, every one of them is incredibly beautiful thanks to lovely coastlines and natural scenic beauty. Most beaches in Italy offer calm and serene surroundings, which can be a welcome break, away from chaotic cities, for people living in crowded urban areas. More importantly these beaches are extremely clean and they have to offer beautiful bathing spots. Many tourists come to visit them every year and they are ready to enjoy the wonderful landscape nearby.

The beauty of these little paradises on the earth is decreed by millions of travelers each year commenting on the choices of Trip Advisor, oozing the ranking of the Traveler's Choice Beaches Awards 2013.

#10 place: The beach of Cala Goloritzè is located along Sardinian northeastern coast, in the north of Arbatax. In 1995 it was nominated "Italian National Monument". Currently it is the most photographed beach in the Ogliastra area Goloritzè is certainly one of the most beautiful beaches in the Mediterranean, thanks to the turquoise color that characterizes its waters and its underwater sources that affect color variations. Small white marble stones characterize the beach. Some calcareous rocks rise from the sea to create little islands.

# 9 place: Romantic walk between the sea and the cliffs, beginning at Positano harbor, the beautiful Fornillo beach. Wonderful venue well established among those always looking for a quieter beach. However since the Fornillo beach is close to the downtown of Positano, during summer weekends can get crowded too.

# 8 place: The stunning Brandinchi beach is located between Punta Sabbatino and Capo Codacavallo, along the Olbia - San Teodoro road (Sardinia). It is a wonderful beach, characterized by a very fine sand clear depth, and colour that goes from a light pastel to an intense blue, also called "Tahiti", due to its beauty. Among the dunes it is easy to find wild lilies.

# 7 place: Located in the south of Italy, Calabria region, Tropea is one of the most famous beaches in the area. It is famous for its lovely sands and for the amazing crystal clear waters that are unmatched by any other beach in the world. Tropea is one of those rare beaches on earth that still offers peace and relax characteristics of an unspoiled environment totally away from chaos and pollution.

# 6 place: In San Vito lo Capo, Sicily, lies a golden beach 3 km long, and gently bathed by the clear blue sea. Very beautiful are the calcareous coves and the untouched areas of the Zingaro Nature Reserve.

#5 place: The Tueredda beach lies on the Teulada shores. It is one of the most beautiful beaches of Sardinia Island. Renowned for its fine sand and transparent waters which make it for sure an incredible paradise.

#4 place: In Sestri Levante, a destination for many lovers this is the enchanting Bay of Silence. A super romantic place surrounded by colorful houses constantly kissed by the sun.

# 3 place: The beach of Cala Mariolu is located along the eastern Sardinian coast, in the province of Nuoro. The beach is near Cala Biriola and Goloritzè. This is a beautiful pebble beach dominated by a limestone hilltop covered by wonderful Mediterranean vegetation. The seabed is rocky and suitable for scuba diving. Some rocks emerging from the sea create small islands. The sun shines all day, while the rest of the day the beach is under the shade.

# 2 place: The Pelosa beach is located 2 km from Stintino (Sardinia), at the end of Capo Falcone, near the small fishing village. The sand is exceptionally white and thin. Right in front of the beach it stands the island of Asinara, which was before a penitentiary and now has become a park, incredible destination for many tourists.

# 1 place: The most beautiful beach in Italy is the beach dei Conigli located in the beautiful archipelago of the Pelagie Islands, Lampedusa. The sand is white, the sea is crystal clear, this and much more, attract visitors and tourists from all over the world. The whole island, in fact, is a nature reserve. Legambiente supervises it because every year in late spring, dozens of sea turtles, protected species, emerge to lay their eggs on the beach, this is an amazing event.
