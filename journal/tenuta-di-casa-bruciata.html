Tenuta di Casa Bruciata

Umbria10 October 2025

Why it’s special In the Umbrian hills near Preggio and Umbertide, Country Italian Wedding is a restored stone hamlet with lawns, olive trees and wide valley views. A vine-covered pergola, panoramic pool and a glass-framed Orangery keep every moment close to nature. The mood is rustic, intimate and relaxed—perfect for a multi-day celebration.

Ideal for A house-party wedding with your core group staying on site. Apartments and suites cluster around the hamlet, so friends meet in courtyards and gardens. Distances are simple: Perugia, Assisi and Lake Trasimeno sit within easy reach for portraits and gentle excursions. We coordinate rooming, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic ceremony in the panoramic garden, among the olives or in the historic villa garden. If you prefer a legal rite, we arrange town-hall or church ceremonies nearby and time the return for cocktails at sunset. Then it’s golden-hour drinks by the pool and a long-table dinner under lights. Should the forecast change, the Orangery provides an elegant, view-connected Plan B. We curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers—so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout As planning benchmarks: the Orangery seats about 96–100 with the house tables and chairs; outdoors we scale comfortably on lawns and terraces, with marquee options when you need full cover. We zone the day—ceremony, aperitivo, dinner, dancing—to keep moves short and sightlines clean for photos.

Good to know You’re on a working countryside estate listed under “Società Agricola Casa Bruciata” in Preggio. The hamlet offers multiple ceremony spots, a long pool for day-after lounging and flexible spaces for pizza night or a relaxed brunch. We manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout, so the weekend stays calm and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: 9 apartments for a total of 37 guests (including sofa beds and extra beds)

Max wedding guests: Maximum 80-90 wedding guests
