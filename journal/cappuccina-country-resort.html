Cappuccina Country Resort

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: set on a hillside a short drive from San Gimignano, Cappuccina Country Resort combines an Art Nouveau villa, sweeping lawns and a panoramic pool that frames the town’s medieval towers. The resort adds a spa spirit to the Tuscan-country feel, with wellness facilities and generous terraces that open directly to the gardens, so every moment flows indoors and out without losing the view.

Ideal for: a multi-day destination celebration where your inner circle stays on site and everyone else nearby. With guestrooms clustered around the main villa and grounds, this is a true house-party venue that can scale for larger receptions outdoors while keeping the atmosphere relaxed. As a planning benchmark, independent venue guides note alfresco capacities up to around 250, while the resort’s indoor hall can be configured as two sections of roughly seventy seats each for an elegant, all-weather fallback. We coordinate room blocks and guest logistics so the weekend feels seamless from arrival to farewell brunch

Wedding experience: think vows on the panoramic terrace with the towers in the distance, a golden-hour aperitivo by the pool and a candle-lit dinner that carries into dancing under the stars, with indoor salons ready if the weather turns. On site we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and décor with our trusted suppliers; for the ceremony itself, legal civil rites are classically held at San Gimignano’s Palazzo Comunale in the frescoed Sala di Dante, while Catholic masses are celebrated in town at the Collegiata or Sant’Agostino. We manage the paperwork, church liaison and timings so everything meshes with your event flow back at the resort.

Good to know: the setting is purpose-built for weddings, with terraces, gardens and an illuminated pool that reads beautifully in photographs day and night. The spa mood—sauna, hammam and an indoor pool—keeps guests happily anchored on property between events, and the short hop into San Gimignano makes off-site portraits and church ceremonies effortless. Florence and Pisa are the natural gateway airports; we handle transfers and scheduling to keep arrivals smooth.

Accommodation: 35 Classic rooms, 16 Superior rooms, 4 Deluxe Rooms, 6 Terrace Suites. Sleeping a total of 150 guests All rooms have a private balcony overlooking the valley or the Villa internal gardens.

Max wedding guests: Up to 250 guests
