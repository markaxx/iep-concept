Il Salviatino

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special Above Florence in Fiesole, Il Salviatino is a 15th-century villa with terraced Italian gardens and long city views. Stone stairways, lawns and a panoramic pool create an easy indoor–outdoor flow. Inside, frescoes and salons keep the historic soul while feeling effortless for modern events. You are minutes from the Duomo, yet the mood stays secluded and green.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. There are about 39 rooms and suites, so your inner circle can stay on site while other guests lodge nearby. Travel is simple: roughly 10 minutes to Florence’s center and about 25 minutes to FLR airport. We coordinate rooming lists, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a civil or symbolic ceremony on the garden terraces with Florence as your backdrop. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights. If the forecast shifts, salons, the library and view verandas keep the timeline smooth without losing atmosphere. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Ceremony: upper terraces or lawns with city views. Aperitivo: parterre gardens and pool deck. Dinner: gravel terraces for alfresco layouts; heritage salons as an elegant Plan B. Overnights: approx. 39 keys across rooms and suites. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know You’re on a historic estate with botanical grounds, a pool and a small spa feel—ideal for a relaxed welcome day and a calm morning after. Distances are short for portraits in town or a church ceremony in Florence. Meanwhile, tastings and chef-led dinners root the weekend in place. We handle permits, paperwork and production throughout, so the rhythm stays unhurried and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps up to 80 guests

Max wedding guests: Ideal for up to 100 guests
