Antica Fattoria Paternò

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special In the Chianti hills near Montespertoli, Antica Fattoria di Paterno crowns a ridge of vineyards, olives and woodland. Stone loggias, courtyards and a panoramic pool frame long valley views. Inside, elegant halls open to the gardens, so every moment flows indoors and out without losing the landscape. The seventeenth-century tower adds character and a private, old-world feel.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding with your inner circle close by. The estate offers a small set of suites in the historic tower for the core group, while other guests stay in nearby accommodations. Travel is easy from Florence and Pisa, and transfers slot neatly into a weekend plan. We coordinate rooming, logistics and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Civil ceremonies are licensed on site, which keeps the timeline smooth. You can hold the rite in the gardens, beneath the glass pergola, or on a terrace with the hills as your backdrop. Then it’s golden-hour cocktails by the pool and a long-table dinner under lights. If the forecast shifts, the main indoor hall keeps the atmosphere intact with its “starry sky” ceiling and wide opening windows. We curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and layout Indoors, the principal hall seats up to about 180 guests, and it links directly to outdoor spaces for a seamless flow. Outdoors, receptions can scale to large formats across courtyards and terraces, with layouts right-sized to your guest count and season. We place ceremony, aperitivo, dinner and dancing in distinct zones to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The arrival along cypress lines sets a cinematic tone, while sunset portraits around the pool and pergola read beautifully. The property hosts weddings across the season, and real-wedding galleries confirm flexible styling—from classic garden dinners to modern lounge setups. Meanwhile, we manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout, so the rhythm stays calm and unhurried

Accommodation: Accommodation comprises of 7 rooms with 14 beds that can host up to 14 people

Max wedding guests: Up to 200 guests
