girl power, your wedding memories!

8 April 2013

I happened to look at the work of two amazing photographers. Two women, two Italians, two artists so talented, we have decided to share their amazing work with you all. Itaian Event Planners is happy to introduce you:

"Women in Wedding Photographers"

Born in Italy, our native land, world country of elegance and design excellence and and develops in the United Arab Emirates, innovative and luxurious new country.

Telling stories capturing emotions has always been a great passion. The love of photography and the images takes us very far, in secret places where there is the happy freedom of expression. We like to tell stories that can be stored in the current time in the memory of those who lived, recalling the moments more real, that smell of sweetness.

Women in Wedding is a project. It's an idea born with the intent to suggest an elegant and very feminine style, which privileges the world of women and the complexities inherent in every gesture, giving a special light to all the thin details that must not fade.

Always give attention to the many cultures of this world who inspire us in every photo shoot and give us the opportunity to meet people’s emotions, creating a stylish and sophisticated reportage of the event.
