Tenuta Corbinaia

Tuscany6 October 2025

Set on a rise among the Chianti hills near Montespertoli, Tenuta Corbinaia is a 19th-century farmhouse turned elegant event estate, wrapped in organic vineyards and olive groves that stretch across roughly forty hectares. The feel is quietly grand: cypress-lined avenues, sculpted gardens, a panoramic pool and a sequence of salons that open to terraces with long Tuscan views. It’s a private, self-contained setting that keeps your guests together while still feeling expansive

This venue shines for multi-day destination weddings with an intimate house-party rhythm. On site you can sleep about sixteen to seventeen guests across eight bedrooms, with additional rooms easily arranged in nearby hotels and agriturismi. Florence Airport is the natural gateway at roughly thirty-five minutes, with Pisa about forty-five, keeping travel smooth for international invitees. Exclusive use is standard and the estate is open year-round, so you can lean into spring gardens, golden summer evenings or the warm glow of the interiors in shoulder season.

Spaces flow naturally from the Italianate gardens to the chandeliers and wrought-iron of the belvedere terraces, then indoors to characterful reception halls. The glass-framed “Crystal” winter garden seats up to about 130 and connects directly to the grounds, while additional halls such as the Sala Rossa and Sala Camino let you tailor the atmosphere by moment. As a planning benchmark, indoor fallback typically seats around 100–130 depending on the room layout, with terraces above offering the same scale when the weather cooperates

Civil ceremonies can be held on site, with symbolic rites framed by the loggias, rose-draped arches and valley views; the estate’s lawns and courtyards are designed for cocktails at sunset and dinner under the stars without losing the refined feel. Trusted external caterers handle banqueting, and the supplier list is curated for quality, while accessibility is good for guests who need it. Music outdoors generally winds down around 11 p.m., with the possibility to extend inside toward 2 a.m. by arrangement.

Accommodation: Sleeps 16 guests in 8 bedrooms

Max wedding guests: Up to 130 guests
