Belmond Hotel Caruso Wedding in Ravello: An Iconic Amalfi Coast Experience

Perched high above the Amalfi Coast, Caruso, A Belmond Hotel is one of Ravello’s most distinctive luxury wedding venues. Set within a historic palazzo surrounded by Mediterranean gardens, the hotel combines Italian heritage, refined hospitality and extraordinary views of the coastline.

For couples dreaming of a Belmond Hotel Caruso wedding, this remarkable property offers the opportunity to create an intimate celebration or an extended wedding experience in one of Italy’s most celebrated destinations.

Why Choose Belmond Hotel Caruso for Your Wedding?

An Iconic Infinity Pool and Panoramic Terraces

One of the hotel’s most recognizable features is its spectacular infinity pool, seemingly suspended above the Amalfi Coast.

The panoramic terraces and landscaped gardens provide an extraordinary setting for wedding photography, elegant gatherings and celebrations inspired by the Mediterranean landscape.

The availability of individual spaces for wedding events should always be confirmed with the hotel.

Historic Italian Elegance

Caruso occupies a historic property whose architectural details, gardens and interiors reflect the character of Ravello.

Its combination of traditional Italian craftsmanship and contemporary luxury makes it particularly appealing to couples seeking a sophisticated destination wedding.

Luxury Accommodation for Wedding Guests

With approximately 50 rooms and suites, the hotel can accommodate many of the couple’s closest family members and friends, subject to availability.

For larger wedding parties, additional accommodation can be arranged in nearby properties.
