Castello di Vicarello

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: tucked away in the Maremma countryside of southern Tuscany, Castello di Vicarello is a lovingly restored 12th-century castle run by the Baccheschi Berti family. The estate blends heritage architecture with refined, contemporary design, offering ten suites, a two-bedroom private villa and three serene marble pools set among nearly 40 hectares of vineyards, olive groves and gardens. It feels like a private home with the services of a luxury retreat, and its own award-winning wines and farm-to-table ethos deepen the sense of place.

Ideal for: couples seeking an intimate, high-touch destination wedding with a multi-day house-party vibe. With accommodation for up to about 30 guests on site, your inner circle can stay within the castle and villa while additional guests lodge in nearby properties. Weddings typically require exclusive use and a minimum stay of around three nights, creating time for welcome cocktails, a relaxed pool day and a farewell brunch without rushing the celebration.

Wedding experience: civil ceremonies and blessings can be held on site in fragrant gardens, olive groves and panoramic terraces, while charming Catholic churches in the nearby villages serve religious rites. The culinary program celebrates seasonal Tuscan ingredients, with tastings of the estate’s wines and bespoke menus; between events, guests can join cooking classes, truffle hunts, e-bike excursions and more for a weekend that feels like a shared holiday.

Accommodation: 10 Suites accommodating up to 20 guests (possibility to add 4 guests at an extra cost of 150 euros per night per guest)

Max wedding guests: Up to 180 guests
