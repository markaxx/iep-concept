Tenute del Cerro

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special Between Val d’Orcia and Val di Chiana, Tenute del Cerro centres your celebration at Relais Villa Grazianella, the estate’s hilltop villa. Vineyards and olive trees run to the horizon. Terraces, a panoramic pool and warm, Tuscan interiors create an easy indoor–outdoor flow. Moreover, the working winery adds tastings and a true sense of place.

Ideal for A multi-day destination wedding with a relaxed, house-party rhythm. On site you have roughly 11 rooms including suites at Villa Grazianella, with additional studios and one-bedroom apartments available within the estate. Your inner circle stays together; everyone else sleeps nearby. Travel is simple via Florence, Pisa or Perugia.

Wedding experience Hold a symbolic ceremony among the cypresses or on the lawn with vineyard views. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner by the pool or on the terrace. If you prefer a civil ceremony, we schedule the town-hall rite in Montepulciano and align timings with your reception at the villa. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers.

Capacities and flow As planning benchmarks, the relais hosts weddings from intimate groups up to and beyond about sixty guests, with indoor salons supporting seated layouts and the grounds scaling outdoors when the weather shines. We right-size floor plans to minimize moves and protect the view.

Good to know The estate spans 600 hectares with vineyards, olive groves, a tasting room and restaurant. Relais facilities include the panoramic pool, gardens and event areas that suit welcome drinks, pool days and farewell brunches. Moreover, rooms face the vineyards, which keeps everyone anchored in the landscape. We manage rooming lists, transfers, paperwork and timelines end to end.

Accommodation: In total, the estate can host up to 64 guest, in the following: Villa Grazianella 11 rooms, that can accommodate up to 32 guests; Casale Le Poggiole (launching May 2026) A newly restored building adjacent to the Villa, which has 8 suites that can accommodate up to 16 guests; Fontelellera Apartments 3 apartments, that can accommodate up to 16 guests

Max wedding guests: Up to 250 guests
