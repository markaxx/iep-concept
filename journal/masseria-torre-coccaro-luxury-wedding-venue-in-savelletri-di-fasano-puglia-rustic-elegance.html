Masseria Torre Coccaro – Luxury Wedding Venue in Savelletri di Fasano, Puglia: Rustic Elegance Amidst Olive Groves

Puglia2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Masseria Torre Coccaro, located in the heart of Puglia, offers a perfect blend of rustic charm and luxurious elegance. This historic farmhouse is surrounded by centuries-old olive groves and ancient stone walls, providing an authentic and romantic atmosphere for couples seeking a southern Italy wedding. With its stunning natural beauty and traditional features, Masseria Torre Coccaro is an ideal destination for those looking to celebrate their love in the heart of the Mediterranean.

Ideal for: Couples desiring a traditional yet glamorous wedding surrounded by the timeless beauty of Puglia’s countryside. The venue offers the perfect combination of rustic elegance and luxurious service, ideal for couples who want to immerse themselves in the region’s rich culture, stunning landscapes, and exceptional cuisine. Masseria Torre Coccaro is a wonderful choice for those seeking a Mediterranean wedding experience that combines authentic charm with high-end amenities.

Wedding Experience: Say your vows in the ancient olive grove, or exchange rings inside the beautifully restored farmhouse, which exudes rustic charm and elegance. After the ceremony, treat your guests to a rustic-style banquet featuring local Puglian specialties paired with wines from the estate’s own vineyards. The venue offers the perfect setting for an intimate, yet glamorous celebration that blends traditional Mediterranean elements with modern luxury. With its impeccable service and breathtaking surroundings, Masseria Torre Coccaro ensures an unforgettable Puglia wedding experience.
