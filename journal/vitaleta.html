Vitaleta

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the heart of the Val d’Orcia, Vitaleta pairs its iconic chapel with terraced gardens and open-sky views that run to the horizon. The mood is timeless and cinematic. Moreover, the adjacent podere offers panoramic dining spaces and refined interiors, so every moment stays close to the landscape.

Ideal for: An intimate-to-mid-size destination wedding with a house-party rhythm. Your core group gathers around the chapel and podere, while additional guests stay nearby. Travel is simple via Florence or Pisa, and the countryside setting keeps the weekend unhurried and photogenic. We coordinate rooming, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience: Exchange vows beside the chapel with the UNESCO hills as your backdrop. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner on the lawns or terraces. If the season calls for it, we shape an elegant indoor plan that preserves the view. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays consistent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and flow: The chapel hosts religious rites indoors for up to about 20, with outdoor ceremonies and receptions scaling to roughly 180 across the gardens and podere interiors. For larger layouts, we right-size floor plans by terrace and lawn to keep movement minimal and sightlines clear.

Good to know: Vitaleta includes a gourmet restaurant space and a casual ristoro next to the chapel—useful for tastings, welcome drinks and day-after brunch. Light is glorious at sunrise and sunset, which reads beautifully in photos across the cypress-lined horizon. Meanwhile, we manage all paperwork, church liaison and supplier timelines, so the day flows

Accommodation: No accommodation on-site

Where to stay: Vitaleta is a location for weddings only. Their sister property Locanda in Tuscany has 9 rooms (max occupancy 24 guests). Rocca d’Orcia Suites and Villas has 12 rooms and 6 apartments/villas (max occupancy 49 guests)
