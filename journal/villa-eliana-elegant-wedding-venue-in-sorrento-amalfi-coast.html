Villa Eliana – Elegant Wedding Venue in Sorrento, Amalfi Coast

Amalfi Coast2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Villa Eliana is a stunning private wedding villa located just outside Sorrento, offering a secluded and romantic venue for couples looking for a luxurious wedding with panoramic views of the Bay of Naples. Surrounded by lush gardens, this historic villa combines old-world charm with modern luxury, making it one of the most sought-after wedding venues on the Amalfi Coast. The intimate setting and beautifully maintained gardens create an unforgettable atmosphere for your special day.

Ideal for: Couples seeking a private, elegant wedding with stunning views of the Bay of Naples and the Amalfi Coast, just minutes from Sorrento. Villa Eliana is perfect for couples who want an intimate wedding experience combined with the beauty and luxury of a high-end destination.

Wedding Experience: Exchange vows in the villa’s lush gardens or on the terrace, where you’ll enjoy sweeping views of the sparkling Bay of Naples. After the ceremony, celebrate in the villa’s elegant indoor dining areas or move outside to the terrace, where guests can indulge in exquisite Mediterranean cuisine and savor fine wines. The private, tranquil atmosphere of Villa Eliana ensures a personalized and luxurious wedding experience, offering a unique Amalfi Coast venue for those seeking an unforgettable wedding day.
