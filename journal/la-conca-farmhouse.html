La Conca Farmhouse

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special: In the hills above Sansepolcro, La Conca spans a private 300-hectare estate of woods, meadows and wide valley views. Stone farmhouses stand alone on their own ridges, each with a private pool and outdoor dining spaces. There’s also a small spa and gym, so the weekend feels like a relaxed house party with wellness time built in.

Ideal for: A multi-day destination wedding where everyone actually slows down. The estate groups several independent villas—such as Villa Cungi and Villa Santarsa—plus apartments and rooms. Your inner circle can stay on site while additional guests lodge nearby. Travel is straightforward via Florence, Pisa or Perugia, with an easy final approach to the property.

Wedding experience: Hold a symbolic ceremony on the lawns, in the oak woods, or beside a panoramic pool. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under lights, with pizza-oven moments woven in for a Tuscan touch. For legal civil paperwork or a Catholic mass, we schedule town-hall or church rites in and around Sansepolcro and align the timeline with your celebrations back at the estate.

Good to know: Each villa offers its own pool, barbecue and generous indoor dining rooms—great for tastings or a cosy plan B. The wellness area includes a jacuzzi with countryside views and a compact gym for a gentle reset between events. We coordinate catering, bar, lighting, music, transport and guest plans end-to-end, so the style stays consistent from arrival to farewell brunch.
