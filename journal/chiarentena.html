Chiarentena

Tuscany8 October 2025

Why it’s special In the heart of the Val d’Orcia, Chiarentana is an ancient fortified farm surrounded by fields and long horizons. Stone courtyards, gardens and a panoramic pool shape an easy indoor–outdoor flow. The estate sits on sister land to La Foce, which adds history and depth to the setting. It feels rustic, elegant and deeply Tuscan.

Ideal for A multi-day, house-party wedding. Restored farmhouses and apartments keep your inner circle on site, while other guests sleep nearby. As planning benchmarks, venue guides and the estate note multiple units for stays, with weddings suited to intimate and mid-size counts. Travel is simple via Florence, Pisa or Perugia. We coordinate rooming plans, transfers and pacing end to end.

Wedding experience Hold a civil or symbolic ceremony on the lawn or in the stone courtyard with the valley behind you. Then move into golden-hour cocktails and a long-table dinner under the trees. If the forecast shifts, we pivot layouts to covered or indoor spaces without losing atmosphere. Throughout, we curate catering, bar, lighting, music and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays consistent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and layout Ceremony: courtyard under the linden and view lawns. Aperitivo: garden alleys beside the main house. Dinner: gravel terrace or courtyard for alfresco layouts; adjacent interiors for a refined Plan B. Overnights: apartments and houses grouped around the borgo keep everyone close. We right-size floor plans to reduce moves and protect sightlines for photos.

Good to know The pool looks across the hills and sits near shaded lawns—ideal for a relaxed day-before or day-after. Tennis and broad gardens add gentle activities between events. Meanwhile, the estate’s scale offers calm, privacy and timeless views for portraits. We manage timelines, paperwork and production throughout, so the weekend stays unhurried and beautifully paced.

Accommodation: Sleeps 32 people in 7 apartments and houses

Max wedding guests: Up to 90 guests
