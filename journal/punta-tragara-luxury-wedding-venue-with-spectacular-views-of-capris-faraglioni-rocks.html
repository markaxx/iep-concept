Punta Tragara – Luxury Wedding Venue with Spectacular Views of Capri’s Faraglioni Rocks

Amalfi Coast2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Punta Tragara is one of the most prestigious and exclusive wedding venues on Capri. Perched atop a cliff, this stunning location offers breathtaking panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea, the iconic Faraglioni Rocks, and the sparkling waters that surround the island. With its rich history, sophisticated architecture, and unparalleled beauty, Punta Tragara provides a perfect blend of romance and elegance for a once-in-a-lifetime celebration.

Ideal for: Couples dreaming of an intimate, luxurious wedding in one of the most picturesque locations in the world. Punta Tragara’s panoramic sea views and proximity to Capri’s famous landmarks make it an ideal choice for those seeking an exclusive, romantic venue with exceptional service.

Experience: Exchange vows at Punta Tragara in one of the stunning outdoor spaces, such as the terrace with unobstructed views of the Faraglioni Rocks, or in the lush Mediterranean garden, surrounded by fragrant flowers and the soothing sound of the waves. After the ceremony, the venue’s chic interiors or outdoor terraces provide an elegant setting for a reception, where guests can enjoy world-class cuisine made from local, fresh ingredients.

For a truly unforgettable experience, host a cocktail reception on one of the outdoor terraces, offering unparalleled views of the island’s coastline, the dramatic Faraglioni Rocks, and the endless Mediterranean. With its sophisticated atmosphere, luxurious service, and iconic location, Punta Tragara offers an unforgettable wedding experience that will leave a lasting impression on both you and your guests.
