Masseria San Domenico – Luxury Wedding Venue in Savelletri di Fasano, Puglia: Rustic Charm with Adriatic Views

Puglia2 December 2024

Why it’s special: Masseria San Domenico is a luxury resort set in an 18th-century farmhouse near the picturesque town of Fasano, Puglia. This stunning venue blends traditional architecture with modern elegance, offering expansive grounds surrounded by centuries-old olive groves, fragrant gardens, and breathtaking views of the Adriatic coast. It’s the perfect destination for couples looking for a romantic and luxurious wedding in the heart of the Puglian countryside.

Ideal for: Couples seeking a perfect blend of rustic charm and luxury for their wedding day. Masseria San Domenico offers an idyllic setting for those who want to experience the beauty and tranquility of the Puglian countryside, combined with top-tier luxury service and relaxation. Whether you’re hosting an intimate ceremony in the gardens or a grand reception on the terrace, this venue offers a memorable and exclusive experience.

Wedding Experience: Exchange vows in the lush, beautifully landscaped gardens, surrounded by century-old olive trees. After the ceremony, celebrate on the stunning terrace, overlooking the Adriatic Sea, where you and your guests can indulge in local Puglian delicacies and wines sourced from the region. For the ultimate wedding experience, enjoy spa services to relax and rejuvenate before your special day. With its combination of natural beauty, luxurious service, and exclusive atmosphere, Masseria San Domenico provides the perfect setting for a dream wedding in Puglia.
