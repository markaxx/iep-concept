Terre di Nano

Tuscany6 October 2025

Why it’s special: in the UNESCO-listed Val d’Orcia between Pienza and Montepulciano, Terre di Nano is a 19th-century country estate wrapped in vineyards, olive groves and rolling hills. The feel is rustic-elegant and warmly personal, with a panoramic pool and an on-site restaurant where the team serves seasonal Tuscan menus and the estate’s own wines and olive oil. The views are the star—ceremony backdrops sweep across the valley in every direction

Ideal for: couples planning an intimate, multi-day destination celebration with their closest guests staying on site. The property sleeps up to about 36 people across the main villa and three independent houses—La Casetta, La Fornace and La Terrazza—plus a handful of apartments and rooms. For guest counts, the venue shines from roughly 30 to 70 for an alfresco dinner, keeping the house-party vibe while still feeling spacious.

Wedding Experience: symbolic ceremonies unfold on the lawn or in the olive garden with widescreen valley views, while civil or religious rites are easily arranged in nearby town halls and churches around Pienza and Montepulciano. After golden-hour cocktails, dinner typically flows under strings of lights, with in-house planning support from Giorgio & Ilaria to shape the weekend. Between events, guests can join cooking classes, wine tastings, Vespa or e-bike outings for a relaxed Tuscan escape. A marquee is the sensible wet-weather fallback in shoulder months.

With its blend of heartfelt hospitality, honest food and cinematic landscapes, Terre di Nano delivers a memorably authentic Val d’Orcia wedding experience

Accommodation: Sleeps 30 guests in 15 rooms

Max wedding guests: Up to 120 guests
