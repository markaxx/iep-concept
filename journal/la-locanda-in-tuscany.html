La Locanda in Tuscany

Tuscany7 October 2025

Why it’s special In the UNESCO-listed Val d’Orcia, Locanda in Tuscany sits among wheat fields and rolling hills. The look is rustic yet refined. Stone buildings, a panoramic pool and wide lawns frame horizon views in every direction. The setting feels cinematic, but also calm and private.

Ideal for A multi-day destination wedding with a true house-party rhythm. The property offers nine rooms and suites, so your inner circle can stay on site while other guests lodge nearby. Travel between events stays simple and unhurried. We coordinate rooming lists, transfers and pacing from arrival to farewell brunch.

Wedding experience Exchange vows in the gardens, under the pool pergola, or in the grain fields with the valley as your backdrop. Legal civil ceremonies are possible on site, which keeps the timeline smooth. After golden-hour cocktails, dinner flows outdoors under lights; in shoulder months we pair the plan with an elegant indoor fallback. We curate catering, music, lighting and styling with our trusted suppliers, so the look stays coherent from aisle to last dance.

Capacities and flow Outdoors, celebrations can scale up to around 180 guests. Indoors, the estate’s Taverna di Mozart seats roughly 60, useful for tastings or an intimate Plan B. We right-size floorplans across lawns, courtyard and terrace to reduce moves and protect the view.

Good to know Exclusive buyouts are available and work well over two or three nights. Between events, guests can unwind by the pool or explore the nearby villages. Meanwhile, rooms feature traditional materials—brick floors, wood beams and stone—so the interiors feel honest and Tuscan. We manage timelines, paperwork and supplier teams end to end

Accommodation: The house sleeps up to 24 guests in 9 rooms, including extra beds

Max wedding guests: Wedding guests capacity – from 50 to 150 guests (the outdoor area is suitable also for higher number of people)
